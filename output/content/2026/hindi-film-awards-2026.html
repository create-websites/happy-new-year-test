Hindi Film Awards 2026: National Awards, IIFA, Screen and Zee Cine Winners

"Article 370" won the National Award for Best Feature Film, "Dhurandhar" swept IIFA with 12 awards, and "Homebound" took Best Film at the Screen Awards. Here are the winners from India's main Hindi film awards in 2026.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated November 17, 2026 · Awards held in 2026

Highlights of the 2026 awards

Filter by ceremony to see the big wins and the stories behind them.

National Film AwardsBest Film

"Article 370" wins Best Feature Film at the 72nd National Film Awards

The Hindi political thriller took the top prize. Its star Yami Gautam won Best Actress and Shashwat Sachdev won Best Music Director for songs. The winners were announced in New Delhi on July 18, 2026, and cover films certified in 2024.

Announced July 18, 2026

National Film AwardsBest Actor

Kartik Aaryan and Mammootty share Best Actor

Kartik Aaryan won for "Chandu Champion", sharing the honour with Malayalam star Mammootty for "Bramayugam". Esquire India calls it Aaryan's first Best Actor National Award.

A shared prize

National Film AwardsHindi film

"Srikanth" is Best Hindi Film; Sanjay Mishra and Randeep Hooda also win

Rajkummar Rao's "Srikanth" won Best Hindi Film. Sanjay Mishra took Best Supporting Actor for "Bhakshak", and Randeep Hooda won Best Debut Director for "Swatantrya Veer Savarkar".

Three Hindi-film wins

National Film AwardsLifetime honour

Anant Nag receives the Dadasaheb Phalke Award

The veteran Kannada actor received India's highest cinema honour from President Droupadi Murmu at the ceremony in Ekta Nagar, Gujarat, on September 22, 2026. The award was for the year 2024.

Presented September 22

National Film AwardsSouth cinema

South cinema takes the craft awards

Tamil film "Amaran" won Best Director for Rajkumar Periasamy, while "Kalki 2898 AD" won the popular-film award. Esquire India notes that Hindi films won the headline prizes but southern films dominated direction, writing and craft categories.

Best Director: Amaran

IIFADhurandhar

"Dhurandhar" sweeps IIFA 2026 with 12 awards

Aditya Dhar's spy drama won Best Picture, Best Director, Best Actor (Ranveer Singh), Best Supporting Actor (Sanjay Dutt), Best Negative Role (Akshaye Khanna) and several writing and technical awards.

12 awards

IIFASaiyaara

"Saiyaara" wins Best Actress and the music prizes

Aneet Padda won Best Actress, Irshad Kamil won Best Lyrics, Faheem Abdullah and Shreya Ghoshal won the playback singing awards, and Ahaan Panday won Best Male Debut, all for "Saiyaara".

Aneet Padda: Best Actress

IIFATechnical

"Chhaava" wins the technical prizes

A.R. Rahman won Best Background Score for "Chhaava", which also took Best Sound Mixing and Best Visual Effects.

Rahman wins score

IIFAFormat

Announced online, with no physical ceremony

The 26th IIFA winners were announced on October 30, 2026, without an on-ground event. The digital awards went to "Baramulla" for Best Picture and "The Ba***ds of Bollywood" for Best Web Series. The 27th IIFA is planned for Abu Dhabi on January 22–23, 2027.

26th edition

Screen AwardsBest Film

"Homebound" wins Best Film at the Screen Awards

Neeraj Ghaywan's film beat "Dhurandhar", "Haq", "Saiyaara" and others for Best Film, and Shalini Vatsa won Best Supporting Actress for it. "Dhurandhar" led the nominations with 24.

Held April 5, 2026

Screen AwardsDhurandhar

"Dhurandhar" still dominates acting, direction and craft

Aditya Dhar won Best Director and Ranveer Singh Best Actor. Akshaye Khanna won Best Supporting Actor, and the film also won action, background score, cinematography, dialogue and editing. Yami Gautam Dhar won Best Actress for "Haq".

Dhar and Singh win

Screen AwardsTribute

Dharmendra honoured with a posthumous Lifetime Achievement Award

The actor's Lifetime Achievement Award was accepted by Bobby Deol.

Accepted by Bobby Deol

Zee Cine AwardsZee Cine

Zee Cine Awards 2026: "Chhaava" tops a split of jury and audience prizes

The 24th Zee Cine Awards were held at the Dome SVP Stadium in Mumbai on March 1, 2026. Akshay Kumar won Best Actor, Kriti Sanon Best Actress, and Vicky Kaushal the Viewer's Choice actor award for "Chhaava".

24th edition

FilmfareLatest edition

Filmfare: the latest Hindi edition was the 70th, in October 2025

The 70th Filmfare Awards were held on October 11, 2025, in Ahmedabad, with "Laapataa Ladies" winning a record-equalling 13 awards. I found no Hindi-language Filmfare ceremony held in 2026 so far; Filmfare Awards South took place in Kochi in February 2026.

Ceremony facts

National Film Awards

The 72nd National Film Awards cover films certified in 2024. An 11-member jury led by filmmaker Jayaraj chose the feature-film winners, announced in New Delhi on July 18, 2026. President Droupadi Murmu presented them at Ekta Nagar, Gujarat, on September 22, 2026.

IIFA

The 26th IIFA winners were announced on October 30, 2026 through social media, with no physical ceremony. The 27th IIFA Weekend & Awards is set for Etihad Arena, Yas Island, Abu Dhabi, on January 22–23, 2027.

Screen Awards

The Screen Awards returned to Mumbai in 2026 after a break. "Dhurandhar" led the nominations with 24, ahead of "Saiyaara" with 17 and "Homebound" with 15.

Zee Cine Awards

The 24th Zee Cine Awards took place at the Dome SVP Stadium in Mumbai over February 28 to March 1, 2026, with winners announced on March 1.

Filmfare

The 70th Filmfare Awards honoured Hindi films of 2024 on October 11, 2025, at EKA Arena in Ahmedabad. "Laapataa Ladies" led with 24 nominations and won 13 awards.

Frequently asked questions

Who won Best Actor at the 2026 National Film Awards?

Kartik Aaryan won Best Actor for "Chandu Champion" and shared the award with Mammootty for "Bramayugam" at the 72nd National Film Awards, announced on July 18, 2026.

Which film won Best Feature Film at the National Film Awards 2026?

The Hindi film "Article 370" won Best Feature Film, and Yami Gautam won Best Actress for it. "Srikanth" won Best Hindi Film.

Who won Best Film, Best Actor and Best Actress at IIFA 2026?

"Dhurandhar" won Best Picture, Ranveer Singh won Best Actor, and Aneet Padda won Best Actress for "Saiyaara" at the 26th IIFA.

How many awards did Dhurandhar win at IIFA 2026?

"Dhurandhar" won 12 awards at IIFA 2026, including Best Picture, Best Director for Aditya Dhar and Best Actor for Ranveer Singh.

Who won the Screen Awards 2026?

"Homebound" won Best Film. Aditya Dhar won Best Director and Ranveer Singh Best Actor for "Dhurandhar", and Yami Gautam Dhar won Best Actress for "Haq".

Who won the Zee Cine Awards 2026?

At the 24th Zee Cine Awards on March 1, 2026, Akshay Kumar won Best Actor and Kriti Sanon won Best Actress. The list names "Chhaava" Best Film, though one outlet reports "Saiyaara" won the viewers' choice film award.

Have the 2026 Filmfare Awards for Hindi films been held?

I could not find a Hindi-language Filmfare ceremony held in 2026 as of November 17, 2026. The latest was the 70th, on October 11, 2025, where "Laapataa Ladies" won 13 awards.

Who received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award in 2026?

Veteran Kannada actor Anant Nag received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2024 at the 72nd National Film Awards ceremony on September 22, 2026.

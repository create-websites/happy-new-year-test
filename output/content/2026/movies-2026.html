The Best and Biggest Movies of 2026: English, Hindi and More

Five Hollywood films passed $1 billion, a $750,000 horror film became a phenomenon, Hindi cinema crossed ₹1,000 crore twice, and Korea, China and South India all had breakout hits. Here is what to watch, and why.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated November 17, 2026 · Figures as of mid-November 2026

Movies everyone should watch

Our picks combine box-office success with critics' reception. They are a starting point, not a ranking.

EnglishSci-fi adventure

Project Hail Mary

Ryan Gosling stars in Phil Lord and Chris Miller's space story, built around a friendship with an alien. It topped several critics' mid-year polls and one poll of 100+ critics called it the only major Oscar contender released by midyear.

$685M worldwide

EnglishEpic

The Odyssey

Christopher Nolan's take on Homer is his biggest film ever and the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time. Best seen on the biggest screen you can find.

$1.8B worldwide

EnglishSuperhero

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

The year's biggest film by far and the third-highest-grossing film of all time. Watch it if you want to know what everyone was talking about.

$2.52B worldwide

EnglishHorror

Obsession

Curry Barker made it in about 20 days for around $750,000, and it grew in its second weekend, which almost never happens in horror. It holds a 95% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes.

$520M+ worldwide

EnglishAnimation

Toy Story 5

The fifth Toy Story film passed $1 billion worldwide, a safe pick for a family movie night.

$1.15B worldwide

EnglishBiopic

Michael

The Michael Jackson biopic passed Oppenheimer to become the highest-grossing biopic ever.

$1.03B worldwide

EnglishDark comedy-drama

The Drama

Kristoffer Borgli's provocative film with Zendaya is known for one of the year's weirdest twists. Go in knowing as little as possible.

HindiSpy thriller

Dhurandhar: The Revenge

Ranveer Singh returns as undercover agent Hamza in the sequel to December 2025's hit. It runs close to four hours and is very violent; critics are split, with ratings from 2 to 4 out of 5, but audiences made it the year's biggest Indian release.

₹1,800 crore+ worldwide

HindiMythological epic

Ramayana: Part 1

Nitesh Tiwari's two-part epic stars Ranbir Kapoor as Ram, Sai Pallavi as Sita and Yash as Ravana. It opened on Diwali weekend and has crossed ₹1,000 crore.

₹1,000 crore+ in days

HindiCrime thriller

Ajay Devgn closes the Vijay Salgaonkar trilogy; it is the biggest hit of his career as a lead. The Malayalam original with Mohanlal is worth comparing.

₹95 crore opening day

HindiWar drama

₹450 crore+ worldwide

₹800 crore+ worldwide

₹378 crore+ worldwide

₹250 crore+ worldwide

The year by language

English / Hollywood

Hollywood: five $1 billion films and a $750,000 horror hit

Five 2026 releases passed $1 billion worldwide: Spider-Man: Brand New Day, The Odyssey, Toy Story 5, Michael and The Super Mario Galaxy Movie. Spider-Man became the third film ever past $2.5 billion. The year's best-reviewed hits were smaller. Project Hail Mary led critics' mid-year polls, and Obsession, made for under $1 million, passed the box-office total of The Exorcist. Some critics called the first half of the year mixed, with low-budget horror doing better than long-running franchises.

Hindi

Hindi cinema: Dhurandhar leads, Ramayana joins the ₹1,000 crore club

Border 2 (Jan 23) gave the year its first blockbuster, and Dhurandhar: The Revenge (Mar 19) became the biggest Indian release of the year by a wide margin. Drishyam 3 (Oct 2) gave Ajay Devgn the biggest opening of his career. Ramayana: Part 1 opened on Nov 6, just before Diwali, and crossed ₹1,000 crore, making it the second Hindi film of 2026 to do so. India's overall box office started slowly, with February the weakest month in three years.

South Indian

South Indian cinema: Rajinikanth's record, and sleeper hits

Tamil cinema had a slow first half, then Jailer 2 (mid-October) became the highest-grossing Tamil film ever. In Telugu, Chiranjeevi's Sankranti hit Mana Shankara Vara Prasad Garu and Ram Charan's Peddi carried a year in which several big-budget releases flopped. Malayalam produced Drishyam 3 with Mohanlal, Vaazha II and Bethlehem Kudumba Unit. In Kannada, Premada Oorali opened to under ₹10 lakh and became only the sixth Kannada film to cross ₹100 crore net.

East Asia

East Asia: Korea's comeback and China's New Year

South Korea's first-half box office rose 41.9% to 579 billion won, led by The Man Who Lives with the King, which became the highest-grossing Korean film by revenue. In China, Pegasus 3 dominated the Lunar New Year season, helping China overtake North America as the world's biggest single film market early in the year. In October, the Japanese animation Chiikawa topped a Korean holiday weekend by 215 viewers over the Korean film Assassin(s).

Frequently asked questions

What is the highest-grossing movie of 2026?

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the highest-grossing film of 2026, with about $2.52 billion worldwide as of mid-November. The Odyssey is second at about $1.8 billion.

What is the highest-grossing Hindi movie of 2026?

Dhurandhar: The Revenge, released on March 19, grossed about ₹1,800 crore worldwide. Ramayana: Part 1, released on November 6, has crossed ₹1,000 crore and was still running when this page was updated.

Which 2026 movies should everyone watch?

Our shortlist spans Hollywood (Project Hail Mary, The Odyssey, Obsession), Hindi (Dhurandhar: The Revenge, Ramayana: Part 1, Drishyam 3), South Indian (Jailer 2, Premada Oorali), and world cinema (The Man Who Lives with the King, Fjord). Use the filters on this page to browse by language.

Which film won the Palme d'Or in 2026?

Cristian Mungiu's Fjord won the Palme d'Or at the 79th Cannes Film Festival on May 23, 2026.

Will this page be updated?

Yes. It covers releases through mid-November 2026, and December releases and final year-end totals will be added later.

The 2026 Oscars: Every Winner from the 98th Academy Awards

One Battle After Another took Best Picture and six Oscars, Sinners won four after a record 16 nominations, and KPop Demon Hunters made K-pop history. Here are the highlights and the full winners list.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated November 17, 2026 · Ceremony of March 15, 2026

Highlights of the night

The big wins, the firsts and the surprises.

Top awardsBest Picture

One Battle After Another wins Best Picture

Paul Thomas Anderson's comic, multi-generational saga of political resistance takes the top prize and leads the night with six Oscars. It arrived as the favourite, with 13 nominations.

6 wins from 13 nominations

Top awardsBest Director

Paul Thomas Anderson finally wins

Anderson takes Best Director and Best Adapted Screenplay for One Battle After Another. He had never won an Oscar before this ceremony.

First Oscars of his career

ActingBest Actor

Michael B. Jordan wins Best Actor for Sinners

Jordan wins one of the night's closest races, ahead of Timothée Chalamet, for his double-duty role in Ryan Coogler's vampire epic. It was his first Oscar nomination.

First nomination, first win

ActingBest Actress

Jessie Buckley wins Best Actress for Hamnet

Buckley plays a grieving mother, Agnes, in Chloé Zhao's Hamnet and becomes the first Irish performer to win the category, after earlier wins at the BAFTAs, Golden Globes and Actor Awards.

First Irish winner

ActingSupporting Actress

Amy Madigan wins for Weapons

Madigan's Best Supporting Actress win for Zach Cregger's horror film Weapons comes about four decades after her first Oscar nomination.

Four decades after a first nod

ActingSupporting Actor

Sean Penn wins for One Battle After Another

Penn wins Best Supporting Actor over co-star Benicio del Toro and others, without attending the ceremony.

Won in absentia

CraftCinematography

Autumn Durald Arkapaw makes history

The Sinners cinematographer becomes the first woman to win Best Cinematography in the Academy's 98 years. She asked all the women in the Dolby Theatre to stand during her speech.

First woman winner

CraftOriginal Screenplay

Sinners takes four Oscars

Ryan Coogler wins Best Original Screenplay, and the film also wins Best Actor, Best Cinematography and Best Original Score for Ludwig Göransson.

Record 16 nominations, 4 wins

CraftFrankenstein

Frankenstein wins three craft Oscars

Guillermo del Toro's Frankenstein wins Costume Design (Kate Hawley), Production Design and Makeup and Hairstyling.

3 wins

CraftVisual Effects

Avatar: Fire and Ash wins Visual Effects

Every Avatar film has now won the category. This one beat Best Picture nominees F1 and Sinners, which is rare for a film outside the Best Picture field.

Every Avatar film has won

CraftBest Casting

Best Casting makes its debut

Cassandra Kulukundis wins the first Best Casting Oscar, for One Battle After Another. It is the Academy's first new category in 25 years.

First new category in 25 years

Animation & world cinemaAnimation & Song

KPop Demon Hunters makes K-pop history

The Netflix hit wins Best Animated Feature, and "Golden" becomes the first K-pop song to win Best Original Song. The voices behind HUNTR/X performed it live while the audience waved light sticks.

2 wins, first K-pop Oscar

Animation & world cinemaInternational Feature

Sentimental Value wins International Feature

Joachim Trier's Norwegian family drama wins Best International Feature. The film had nine nominations in all.

Norway's entry wins

Animation & world cinemaDocumentary Feature

Mr. Nobody Against Putin wins the documentary Oscar

The film about a Russian schoolteacher quietly resisting the war in Ukraine beat The Perfect Neighbor, which most media forecasters had picked.

A category upset

Animation & world cinemaLive Action Short

A rare tie in Live Action Short

The Singers and Two People Exchanging Saliva share the prize, only the seventh tie in Oscars history.

Seventh tie ever

Ceremony facts

Ceremony

The 98th Academy Awards were held on March 15, 2026, at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles, with 24 categories. Conan O'Brien hosted for the second year in a row on ABC and Hulu.

Nominations

Nominations were announced on January 22, 2026. Sinners set an all-time record with 16, ahead of the previous high of 14. One Battle After Another had 13; Marty Supreme, Frankenstein and Sentimental Value had nine each; Hamnet had eight.

Audience

The telecast drew 17.86 million viewers, down about 9% from last year's 19.7 million.

What is next

The Oscars are due to move to YouTube from the 101st ceremony in 2029.

Frequently asked questions

Who won Best Picture at the 2026 Oscars?

One Battle After Another won Best Picture at the 98th Academy Awards, held on March 15, 2026. It also won Best Director, Best Adapted Screenplay, Best Supporting Actor, Best Film Editing and Best Casting, for six Oscars in all.

Who won Best Actor and Best Actress at the 2026 Oscars?

Michael B. Jordan won Best Actor for Sinners, and Jessie Buckley won Best Actress for Hamnet, becoming the first Irish performer to win the category.

Who won Best Director at the 2026 Oscars?

Paul Thomas Anderson won Best Director for One Battle After Another. It was his first Oscar win.

Which film won the most Oscars in 2026?

One Battle After Another won the most with six, followed by Sinners with four and Frankenstein with three. KPop Demon Hunters won two.

When were the 2026 Oscars and who hosted?

The 98th Academy Awards took place on March 15, 2026, at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles. Conan O'Brien hosted for the second year in a row, and the show aired on ABC and Hulu.

Did Sinners set a record at the 2026 Oscars?

Yes. Sinners earned 16 nominations, the most for any film in Oscar history, beating the previous record of 14. It won four awards: Best Actor, Original Screenplay, Cinematography and Original Score.

What won Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song?

KPop Demon Hunters won Best Animated Feature, and its song "Golden" won Best Original Song, the first K-pop song to win that award.

The Sports Year of 2026: World Cup, Cricket, F1 and More

Spain won the World Cup, India won the T20 World Cup at home, a 20-year-old became Formula 1's youngest champion, and the Knicks and Brewers ended long waits. Here is the year in sport, with cricket and Indian sport in focus.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated November 17, 2026 · Results as of mid-November 2026

Moments of the year, newest first

Filter by sport to follow the season you care about.

Formula 1Nov 1

Antonelli becomes F1's youngest world champion

Kimi Antonelli clinched the 2026 drivers' title at the Mexico City Grand Prix with a third-place finish, four rounds early. At 20 years and 68 days he broke Sebastian Vettel's 2010 record. In only his second season, he won his first race in China in March and became the youngest-ever points leader after Japan.

380 points at the clinch

US leaguesOct 28

Brewers win their first World Series

Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 4-1 in the series and clinched in Game 5. Catcher William Contreras was named series MVP, and the Brewers lost only two games in the whole postseason.

First title in franchise history

FootballOct 26

Harry Kane wins the Ballon d'Or

Kane beat Kylian Mbappé and Lamine Yamal to become the first Englishman to win since Michael Owen in 2001. Alexia Putellas won the women's award, her third, and Yamal took the Kopa Trophy for a third straight year.

London Palladium ceremony

Multi-sport GamesSep 19 - Oct 4

India finish fourth at the Asian Games

India won 85 medals in Aichi-Nagoya, below the record 106 of Hangzhou but still its second-best haul. The men's hockey team beat Malaysia 5-1 in the final to retain the title, and India earned LA 2028 Olympic quota places in archery and hockey.

21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze

TennisSep 13

Zverev and Rybakina win the US Open

Alexander Zverev beat Ben Shelton in four sets for his second major of the year. Elena Rybakina beat two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka in three sets for her third Grand Slam singles title.

Shelton: first Black American man in a US Open final since 1972

Multi-sport GamesJul 23 - Aug 2

Commonwealth Games in Glasgow

A trimmed programme with 74 teams. Australia topped the table again. India finished fourth with 39 medals, boxing delivered its biggest haul, and Mirabai Chanu won a third straight Games gold. Ahmedabad hosts in 2030.

India: 13 gold, 17 silver, 9 bronze

FootballJul 19

Spain win the World Cup

In the largest World Cup ever, Spain beat defending champions Argentina 1-0 after extra time in New Jersey. Substitute Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute after Argentina went down to ten men. Rodri won the Golden Ball and Mbappé finished as top scorer.

Spain's second title, first since 2010

GolfJul 19

Ryan Fox wins the Open at Royal Birkdale

Fox charged through the weekend and holed a 10-foot putt on the 72nd hole to win the final men's major of the year.

Fourth different major winner of 2026

TennisJul 12

Sinner defends Wimbledon; Noskova wins her first major

Jannik Sinner beat Alexander Zverev 6-7, 7-6, 6-3, 6-4 for a second straight title and his fifth major. A day earlier 21-year-old Linda Noskova beat fellow Czech Karolina Muchova in the first all-Czech Grand Slam final of the professional era.

Noskova: youngest Wimbledon champion since 2011

CricketJul 5

Australia win the Women's T20 World Cup

Australia chased down England's 150 at a sold-out Lord's with seven wickets and 17 balls to spare. Beth Mooney made 64 not out of 49 balls as the chase reached 153 for 3.

Record seventh T20 title

GolfJun 21

Wyndham Clark wins the US Open at Shinnecock Hills

Clark held off a late charge from Sam Burns for his second US Open and second major, after Rory McIlroy and Aaron Rai had won the first two majors.

First American major winner of 2026

US leaguesJun 13-14

Knicks and Hurricanes end long waits

The New York Knicks beat San Antonio 4-1 in the NBA Finals for their first title since 1973, with Jalen Brunson as Finals MVP. A day later the Carolina Hurricanes beat Vegas 4-2 in the Stanley Cup Final for the franchise's second Cup.

Knicks: 53-year wait ended

TennisJun 7

Zverev and Andreeva win the French Open

Alexander Zverev beat Flavio Cobolli in five sets for his first major after three lost finals. Mirra Andreeva, 19, beat qualifier Maja Chwalinska 6-3, 6-3 for her first. The draw was full of early upsets.

Two first-time major champions

CricketMay 31

RCB retain the IPL

Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Gujarat Titans by five wickets in Ahmedabad, with Virat Kohli unbeaten on 75 and hitting the winning six. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi won the Orange Cap with 776 runs at a strike rate above 237.

Back-to-back titles

FootballMay 30

PSG retain the Champions League; Arsenal win the league

Paris Saint-Germain beat Arsenal 4-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw in Budapest to win back-to-back European titles. Arsenal had days earlier won their first Premier League title in 22 years, reaching 82 points with one game left.

Arsenal: 82 points after 37 games

GolfMay

Aaron Rai wins the PGA Championship

Rai won at Aronimink Golf Club and earned a five-year PGA Tour exemption, one of the biggest surprises of the golf year.

Pre-tournament odds under 1%

US leaguesApr 6

Michigan and UCLA win NCAA basketball titles

Michigan beat UConn 69-63 in the men's final. A day earlier UCLA beat South Carolina 79-51 in the women's final.

March Madness

GolfApril

McIlroy wins back-to-back Masters

Rory McIlroy finished 12 under, one shot ahead of Scottie Scheffler, and became the first player to repeat at Augusta since Tiger Woods in 2001-02. It was his sixth major.

Sixth major title

CricketMar 8

India win the T20 World Cup at home

India beat New Zealand by 96 runs in Ahmedabad, making 255 for 5, the highest total in a T20 World Cup final. Sanju Samson hit 89 and was player of the tournament, and Jasprit Bumrah took four wickets to be player of the final.

First team to win it at home and to defend it

Multi-sport GamesFeb 22

Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics close

Norway topped the table again with 18 golds and 41 medals. The United States set a record Winter Games gold haul and beat Canada in overtime in the men's hockey final to close the Games.

Norway 18 gold; USA 12 gold, 33 medals

US leaguesFeb

Seahawks win Super Bowl LX

Seattle beat New England 29-13. Kenneth Walker III ran for 135 yards and became the first running back in 28 years to win Super Bowl MVP, and kicker Jason Myers set a Super Bowl record with five field goals.

Seahawks' second title

TennisJan 31 - Feb 1

Alcaraz completes the career Grand Slam in Melbourne

Carlos Alcaraz beat Novak Djokovic 2-6, 6-2, 6-3, 7-5 at the Australian Open to become the youngest man to win all four majors. Elena Rybakina beat world No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 4-6, 6-4 for her second major.

Alcaraz: seven majors at 22

US leaguesJan 19

Indiana win the college football title

Indiana beat Miami 27-21 in the College Football Playoff final to win the program's first national championship.

First title in program history

Indian sport in 2026

Cricket

India won the men's T20 World Cup at home on March 8 and RCB, captained by Rajat Patidar, retained the IPL on May 31. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi of Rajasthan Royals won the Orange Cap with 776 runs.

Multi-sport Games

India finished fourth at both the Commonwealth Games (39 medals) and the Asian Games (85 medals). Boxing was the star at Glasgow, and the Asian Games brought gold in men's hockey and cricket, plus archery and wrestling medals.

Athletics

Neeraj Chopra returned to the Commonwealth Games and took javelin silver in Glasgow.

What is next

Ahmedabad hosts the 2030 Commonwealth Games, and India has quota places in archery and hockey for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

What to watch next

ATP Finals, Turin (Nov 15-22)

The season-ending tennis championship for the top eight players is under way at the Inalpi Arena on indoor hard courts.

Remaining Formula 1 rounds

Antonelli has the title, but constructors' placings, race wins and second place in the drivers' standings are still being decided over the final rounds.

Men's ODI World Cup 2027

Cricket's 50-over World Cup will be staged in Africa in 2027, and the next men's T20 World Cup is in Australia and New Zealand in 2028.

Los Angeles 2028

India has secured Olympic quota places in archery and hockey. Winter Olympics 2030 will be in the French Alps.

Commonwealth Games 2030

Ahmedabad will host the Games after Glasgow 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who won the 2026 FIFA World Cup?

Spain won the 2026 FIFA World Cup, beating defending champions Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the July 19 final in New Jersey. Substitute Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute, and Rodri won the Golden Ball.

Who won the T20 World Cup 2026?

India won the men's T20 World Cup on March 8, beating New Zealand by 96 runs in Ahmedabad, and became the first team to win the tournament at home and the first to defend the title. Australia won the Women's T20 World Cup on July 5, beating England at Lord's.

Who won IPL 2026?

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won IPL 2026, beating Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the May 31 final in Ahmedabad. It was RCB's second title in a row. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi won the Orange Cap with 776 runs.

Who is the 2026 Formula 1 champion?

Kimi Antonelli of Mercedes won the 2026 Formula 1 drivers' championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix on November 1. At 20 years and 68 days he is the youngest world champion in the sport's history.

Who won the 2026 tennis Grand Slams?

Alcaraz won the Australian Open, Zverev the French Open and US Open, and Sinner Wimbledon. On the women's side Rybakina won in Australia and at the US Open, Andreeva at Roland Garros and Noskova at Wimbledon.

Who won the Super Bowl, NBA Finals, Stanley Cup and World Series in 2026?

The Seattle Seahawks won Super Bowl LX, the New York Knicks won the NBA Finals, the Carolina Hurricanes won the Stanley Cup and the Milwaukee Brewers won the World Series.

How did India do at the 2026 Commonwealth and Asian Games?

India finished fourth at both. At the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow it won 39 medals (13 gold). At the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya it won 85 medals (21 gold), its second-best total ever.

Will this page be updated?

Yes. It covers results through mid-November 2026. The ATP Finals, the final Formula 1 rounds, and year-end awards will be added later.

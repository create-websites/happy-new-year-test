April 7–15

A Season of New Years & Harvest Gratitude

April brings multiple traditions of starting anew. Gudi Padwa and Ugadi welcome a new year in several Indian communities, while Vaisakhi combines harvest celebration with deep significance in Sikh history and faith. Around the same period, Vishu, Bihu and Bengali New Year customs bring their own regional rhythms.

🏠 Home & Decoration

Gudi displays, rangoli, fresh flowers, festive entryways and seasonal home preparation.

🌾 Harvest & Community

Gratitude for crops, community gatherings, music, dance, shared meals and service.

🙏 Faith & Reflection

Temple and gurdwara visits, prayers, readings and traditions centered on renewal and gratitude.

💌 Wishes & Greetings

Share regional New Year greetings, harvest wishes and warm messages for family and friends.

April 22, 2027

Earth Day: Make Spring Renewal Practical

Earth Day is a natural extension of April’s renewal theme. Use the day for small, visible actions: grow something, reduce waste, clean a shared space, spend time outdoors or make one household habit more sustainable.

Grow something

Plant herbs, flowers, native plants or a small container garden with children.

Clean a shared space

Choose a park, trail, neighborhood or apartment common area for a simple cleanup.

Think about food

Reduce food waste, plan meals more carefully and explore seasonal or locally grown ingredients.

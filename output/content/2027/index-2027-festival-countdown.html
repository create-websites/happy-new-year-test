About this page

Dates follow standard calendar rules for fixed and weekday-based holidays and published calendars for moon-based festivals, which can differ by a day. Timers count to midnight at the start of each day in your device time zone. Confirm festival dates locally before making plans. This page is informational only. Created with AI assistance and published under the byline of Happy New Year 2027.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see festivals for my region?

Choose a region at the top of the page. The USA is shown by default, and the list switches to India, the UK, Europe, the Middle East and Türkiye, China, Japan and Korea, Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand, or all regions together. You can narrow the list further by tradition.

How does the countdown work?

Each timer counts down to midnight at the start of the festival day in your device's time zone. On the day itself the card shows a greeting, and after it the card is marked as celebrated.

Which dates can change?

Dates based on the moon, such as Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Ramadan, the Hijri New Year, Holi, Makar Sankranti and the Prophet's Birthday, can differ by a day depending on moon sighting or the calendar used. They are marked as approximate on the cards.

When is Easter in 2027?

Western Easter Sunday is March 28, 2027. Orthodox Easter is on May 2, five weeks later.

When is Diwali in 2027?

Diwali falls on Friday, October 29, 2027, with Dussehra on Saturday, October 9.

When are Mother's Day, Father's Day and Thanksgiving in 2027 in the US?

In the US, Mother's Day is Sunday, May 9, Father's Day is Sunday, June 20, and Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25.

Why does the page need JavaScript?

The live timers and region picker use JavaScript. The festival names and dates are also written in the page, so they are readable without it.

Where can I find public holidays for my country?

The 2027 World Holidays page lets you pick a region, country or month and lists public holidays, long weekends and leave windows.

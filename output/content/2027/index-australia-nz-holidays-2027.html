January 2027

New Year, Australia Day and New Zealand's extra Monday

New Year's Day is Friday, January 1. Australia Day is Tuesday, January 26. New Zealand's Day after New Year's Day falls on Saturday, January 2, so Monday, January 4, is the holiday.

  1. Fri, Jan 1National or state holiday

    New Year's Day is a public holiday in every Australian state and territory and in New Zealand.

  2. Mon, Jan 4Substitute or additional day

    New Zealand's Day after New Year's Day falls on Saturday, January 2, and is observed on Monday, January 4 (Mondayisation).

  3. Wed, Jan 6By state or territory

    Devonport Cup Day is a local holiday in Tasmania on Wednesday, January 6.

  4. Tue, Jan 26National or state holiday

    Australia Day is a public holiday in all Australian states and territories on Tuesday, January 26.

March 2027

Labour Day in WA and Victoria, Canberra Day and Easter

WA has Labour Day on Monday, March 1. Victoria, the ACT, South Australia and Tasmania hold their March holiday on Monday, March 8. Good Friday is March 26.

  1. Mon, Mar 1National or state holiday

    Labour Day is a public holiday in Western Australia on Monday, March 1.

  2. Mon, Mar 8National or state holiday

    A March holiday falls on Monday, March 8, with a different name in each place: Labour Day in Victoria, Canberra Day in the ACT, Adelaide Cup Day in South Australia and Eight Hours Day in Tasmania.

  3. Fri, Mar 26Seasons & events

    Good Friday is a public holiday across Australia and New Zealand.

  4. Sat, Mar 27Substitute or additional day

    The Saturday after Good Friday is a public holiday in most states and territories, but not in Tasmania or Western Australia.

  5. Mon, Mar 29Seasons & events

    Easter Monday is a public holiday across Australia and New Zealand. Easter Tuesday is a restricted holiday in Tasmania on March 30.

April 2027

Anzac Day on a Sunday

Anzac Day falls on Sunday, April 25. Most places add a holiday on Monday, April 26, but Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania do not. New Zealand observes it on Monday, April 26.

  1. Sun, Apr 25National or state holiday

  2. Mon, Apr 26Substitute or additional day

    An additional Anzac Day holiday applies on Monday, April 26, in most places except Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania. New Zealand observes Anzac Day on this Monday. Queensland's Anzac Day is on this date.

Frequently asked questions

When is Australia Day in 2027?

Australia Day is Tuesday, January 26, 2027, and is a public holiday in every state and territory. A Monday leave day gives a four-day weekend.

What changes at Easter in 2027?

Good Friday is March 26 and Easter Monday is March 29. Most states and territories also give the Saturday and some give Easter Sunday, while Tasmania and Western Australia differ.

When is the King's Birthday holiday in 2027?

It is Monday, June 14, in New South Wales, Victoria, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory. Queensland holds it on October 4 and Western Australia on September 27.

What happens when Anzac Day falls on a Sunday?

Anzac Day is on Sunday, April 25, 2027. New South Wales, the ACT, Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory give an extra day on Monday, April 26, while Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania do not.

When is Matariki in 2027?

Matariki is a New Zealand public holiday on Friday, June 25, 2027.

What is Mondayisation?

In New Zealand, when some holidays fall on a weekend they are observed on the following Monday. In 2027 this applies to Waitangi Day and Anzac Day, and Day after New Year's Day is also moved to Monday, January 4.

Which holidays are not covered here?

New Zealand's regional anniversary days, Australian local show days beyond those listed and part-day local holidays are not covered in full. Check your own region.

Is this an official list?

No. Australian dates follow the Fair Work Ombudsman and a public holiday calendar. New Zealand dates follow national rules and should be checked against the official page. Confirm with your employer. This page is informational only.

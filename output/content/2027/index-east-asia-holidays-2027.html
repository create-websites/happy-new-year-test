Frequently asked questions

When is Chinese New Year in 2027, and how many days off do people get?

Chinese New Year is Saturday, February 6, 2027. China's official Spring Festival break runs nine days, February 5-13, with make-up workdays on January 31 and February 14.

How many public holidays does China have in 2027?

China has 13 statutory public holidays in 2027, and the State Council's schedule gives 29 days off in seven periods, with five weekend days made into workdays.

When is Golden Week in Japan in 2027?

Golden Week runs from Thursday, April 29 (Showa Day) to Wednesday, May 5. Friday, April 30, is not a holiday, so one leave day gives a seven-day break.

Is there a Silver Week in Japan in 2027?

No. Respect for the Aged Day (September 20) and Autumnal Equinox Day (September 23) are not adjacent, so there is no five-day Silver Week in 2027.

How many holidays does South Korea have in 2027?

South Korea has 13 public holidays and seven substitute days, which Klook counts as 24 days off. Labor Day and Constitution Day are newer public holidays.

When are Chuseok and Seollal in 2027?

Seollal is February 6-8 with a substitute on February 9. Chuseok is September 14-16, with the main day on September 15 and no substitute.

Which places are not covered here?

Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, Mongolia and North Korea are not listed. Mainland China's local holidays for specific groups are limited to those shown.

Is this an official list?

China's dates follow the State Council notice of November 5, 2026. Other dates come from published calendars and may change. Confirm with your employer or the official source. This page is informational only.

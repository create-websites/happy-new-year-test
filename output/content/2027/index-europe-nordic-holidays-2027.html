January 2027

New Year, and Epiphany in Sweden and Finland

New Year's Day is Friday, January 1, in all four countries. Epiphany on Wednesday, January 6, is a public holiday in Sweden and Finland, but not in Denmark or Norway.

  1. Fri, Jan 1Public holiday

    New Year's Day is a public holiday in Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland, giving a three-day weekend, January 1-3.

  2. Wed, Jan 6Regional or by country

    Epiphany is a public holiday in Sweden and Finland on Wednesday, January 6. It is a working day in Denmark and Norway.

February 2027

Fastelavn and Fat Tuesday, with no public holidays

February has no public holidays. Denmark celebrates Fastelavn on Sunday, February 7, and Fat Tuesday (semla day in Sweden) falls on February 9.

  1. All monthPlan your leave

    No public holiday falls in February 2027 in Denmark, Norway, Sweden or Finland.

  2. Sun, Feb 7Tradition & faith

    Fastelavn, the Danish carnival, falls on Sunday, February 7, the Sunday before Lent.

  3. Tue, Feb 9Tradition & faith

    Fat Tuesday (Fettisdagen) falls on February 9, the day before Ash Wednesday, February 10.

March 2027

Maundy Thursday, Good Friday and Easter, with clocks forward

Easter runs from Maundy Thursday, March 25, to Easter Monday, March 29, and the clocks go forward on Easter Sunday. Denmark and Norway also take Maundy Thursday as a public holiday.

  1. Thu, Mar 25Regional or by country

    Maundy Thursday is a public holiday in Denmark and Norway, but not in Sweden or Finland.

  2. Fri, Mar 26Public holiday

    Good Friday is a public holiday in all four Nordic countries.

  3. Sun, Mar 28Clocks & seasons

    Easter Sunday and the start of summer time fall on the same day: clocks go forward one hour overnight on March 28.

  4. Mon, Mar 29Public holiday

    Easter Monday is a public holiday in all four countries. Easter gives a five-day break in Denmark and Norway, March 25-29, and four days in Sweden and Finland, March 26-29.

May 2027

May Day, Ascension and Norway's Constitution Day

May 1 falls on a Saturday. Ascension is Thursday, May 6, Whit Sunday is May 16, and Norway's Constitution Day on May 17 is also Whit Monday.

  1. Sat, May 1Falls on a weekend

    May Day falls on a Saturday. It is a public holiday in Sweden, Norway and Finland, but not an official one in Denmark, so there is no weekday off.

  2. Thu, May 6Public holiday

    Ascension Day is a public holiday in all four countries on Thursday, May 6. Taking Friday, May 7, off gives a four-day break.

  3. Sun, May 16Public holiday

    Whit Sunday (Pentecost) is May 16 and is a public holiday in all four countries.

  4. Mon, May 17Regional or by country

    Whit Monday is a public holiday in Denmark and Norway but not in Sweden or Finland. In Norway it coincides with Constitution Day, so there is just one day off.

June 2027

Sweden's National Day and Midsummer

Sweden's National Day falls on Sunday, June 6. Midsummer is the biggest summer celebration in Sweden and Finland, on Friday, June 25 and Saturday, June 26.

  1. Sat, Jun 5Falls on a weekend

    Denmark's Constitution Day, on Saturday, June 5, is not an official public holiday.

  2. Sun, Jun 6Falls on a weekend

    Sweden's National Day is a public holiday and falls on a Sunday in 2027.

  3. Fri, Jun 25Unofficial days off

    Midsummer Eve falls on Friday, June 25. It is not an official holiday in Sweden or Finland, but it is treated as a day off for most workers.

  4. Sat, Jun 26Public holiday

    Midsummer Day on Saturday, June 26, is a public holiday in Sweden and Finland.

December 2027

Finland's Independence Day and a Saturday Christmas

Finland celebrates Independence Day on Monday, December 6. Christmas Eve is Friday, December 24, Christmas Day is Saturday, and Boxing Day is Sunday.

  1. Mon, Dec 6Regional or by country

    Finland's Independence Day is a public holiday on Monday, December 6, giving a three-day weekend.

  2. Mon, Dec 13Tradition & faith

    St Lucia Day, celebrated in Sweden, falls on Monday, December 13. It is not a public holiday.

  3. Fri, Dec 24Unofficial days off

    Christmas Eve falls on Friday, December 24. It is not an official public holiday in Sweden or Denmark, but many workplaces give the day off.

  4. Sat, Dec 25Falls on a weekend

    Christmas Day falls on a Saturday and the second day of Christmas on Sunday, December 26, in all four countries, with no weekday substitute.

  5. Fri, Dec 31Unofficial days off

    New Year's Eve falls on Friday, December 31. It is not an official holiday in Denmark or Sweden, but many workplaces give the day off.

Frequently asked questions

Which Nordic holidays are the same in 2027?

New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Ascension Day, Christmas Day and the second day of Christmas are public holidays in Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland. Some differences are Maundy Thursday, Whit Monday, Epiphany and May 1.

Is Maundy Thursday a holiday in 2027?

In Denmark and Norway, yes, on Thursday, March 25. In Sweden and Finland, no. That makes Easter five days in Denmark and Norway and four in Sweden and Finland.

Is May 1 a public holiday in Denmark?

No. Öresunddirekt notes that May 1 and Constitution Day, June 5, are not official public holidays in Denmark, but many employees get a half or full day off.

Are Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve public holidays?

Not officially in Sweden or Denmark. Many workplaces give the days off, and both fall on a Friday in 2027.

When is Midsummer in 2027?

Midsummer Eve is Friday, June 25 and Midsummer Day is Saturday, June 26. It is the main summer celebration in Sweden and Finland.

Which holidays fall on weekends in 2027?

May Day, Whit Sunday, Sweden's National Day, Midsummer Day, All Saints' Day, Christmas Day and the second day of Christmas all fall on weekends.

Which holidays are not covered here?

Iceland, the Faroe Islands, the Baltic states and local or regional holidays are not listed.

Is this an official list?

No. It combines public holiday calendars and employer guides, listed under each month. Some details come from calendars that give dates by rule. Confirm with your employer or the national authority. This page is informational only.

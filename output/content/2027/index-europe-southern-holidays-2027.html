January 2027

New Year and Epiphany, the Three Kings holiday

New Year's Day is Friday, January 1. Epiphany on Wednesday, January 6, is a public holiday in Spain, Italy and Greece but not in Portugal.

  1. Fri, Jan 1Public holiday

    New Year's Day is a public holiday in Spain, Italy, Portugal and Greece, giving a three-day weekend, January 1-3.

  2. Wed, Jan 6Public holiday

    Epiphany (Día de Reyes, Epifania, Theophania) falls on Wednesday, January 6 and is a public holiday in Spain, Italy and Greece. It falls midweek in 2027 and forms no bridge.

February 2027

Carnival, with Portugal's Entrudo on February 9

February has no fixed public holidays in these countries. Carnival Tuesday falls on February 9, and Portugal's government usually decides each year whether to give the day off.

  1. Tue, Feb 9Tradition & faith

    Carnival Tuesday is February 9. It appears on Portugal's list of customary days off, but whether employees get it depends on the government's annual decision.

  2. Until Mar 14Orthodox calendar

    Greece's carnival season (Apokries) runs until Sunday, March 14, ahead of Clean Monday on March 15, the start of Orthodox Lent.

March 2027

Clean Monday, Holy Week and Western Easter

Western Easter is March 28, with Good Friday on March 26, but Greece keeps Orthodox Easter five weeks later. Greece also celebrates Independence Day and the Annunciation on March 25.

  1. Mon, Mar 15Orthodox calendar

    Clean Monday (Kathara Deftera), the start of Orthodox Lent, is a public holiday in Greece on March 15.

  2. Fri, Mar 19Regional or by country

    Saint Joseph's Day, Friday, March 19, is a regional holiday in some Spanish communities. Time Out Madrid cites a San José bridge in its Madrid calendar, so check your community.

  3. Thu, Mar 25Regional or by country

    Holy Thursday is a holiday in most of Spain and not in Italy. In Greece, March 25 is both Independence Day and the Annunciation, both public holidays.

  4. Fri, Mar 26Public holiday

    Good Friday is a national holiday in Spain and a public holiday in Portugal. It is not a national holiday in Italy.

  5. Sun, Mar 28Clocks & seasons

    Western Easter Sunday and the start of summer time fall on the same day: clocks go forward one hour overnight on March 28.

  6. Mon, Mar 29Regional or by country

    Easter Monday (Pasquetta) is a public holiday in Italy. In Spain it is a holiday in some regions only, and it is not a holiday in Portugal or Greece.

May 2027

Orthodox Easter, Labour Day and Corpus Christi

Greece's Orthodox Easter is Sunday, May 2, with Easter Monday on May 3. Labour Day on May 1 falls on Holy Saturday, and Portugal has Corpus Christi on May 27.

  1. Sat, May 1Falls on a weekend

    Labour Day falls on a Saturday in all four countries. One calendar lists a Greek substitute on Tuesday, May 4, so confirm the official decision.

  2. Sun, May 2Orthodox calendar

    Orthodox Easter Sunday is May 2, five weeks after Western Easter. It is the biggest holiday of the Greek year.

  3. Mon, May 3Orthodox calendar

    Orthodox Easter Monday is a public holiday in Greece on May 3.

  4. Thu, May 27Regional or by country

    Corpus Christi is a public holiday in Portugal on Thursday, May 27. Taking Friday, May 28, off gives a four-day weekend.

June 2027

Republic Day, Portugal Day and Greece's Whit Monday

Italy's Republic Day is Wednesday, June 2. Portugal Day is Thursday, June 10, which makes a four-day weekend with Friday off. Greece has Orthodox Whit Monday on June 21.

  1. Wed, Jun 2Public holiday

    Republic Day is a public holiday in Italy on Wednesday, June 2.

  2. Thu, Jun 10Public holiday

    Portugal Day (Dia de Portugal) is a public holiday on Thursday, June 10. Taking Friday, June 11, off gives a four-day weekend.

  3. Mon, Jun 21Orthodox calendar

    Orthodox Whit Monday (Holy Spirit Day) is a public holiday in Greece on June 21.

  4. Municipal daysRegional or by country

    Portugal and Italy have local patron-saint days: Lisbon's St Anthony on June 13 (a Sunday) and Porto's São João on June 24, and Rome's patron saints on June 29. Check your municipality.

October 2027

St Francis in Italy, Republic Day in Portugal, Hispanidad and Ochi Day

October carries four national days: St Francis on Monday, October 4 in Italy, Portugal's Republic Day on October 5, Spain's National Day on October 12 and Greece's Ochi Day on October 28.

  1. Mon, Oct 4Public holiday

    The Feast of St Francis of Assisi is now a national holiday in Italy and falls on Monday, October 4, giving a three-day weekend, October 2-4.

  2. Tue, Oct 5Public holiday

    Portugal's Republic Day is on Tuesday, October 5. Taking Monday, October 4, off gives a four-day break, October 2-5.

  3. Tue, Oct 12Public holiday

    Spain's National Day (Fiesta Nacional de España) is Tuesday, October 12. Taking Monday, October 11, off gives a four-day break, October 9-12.

  4. Thu, Oct 28Public holiday

    Ochi Day is a public holiday in Greece on Thursday, October 28. Taking Friday, October 29, off gives a four-day break.

  5. Sun, Oct 31Clocks & seasons

    Summer time ends and the clocks go back one hour on Sunday, October 31.

December 2027

Immaculate Conception, Constitution Day and Christmas

Spain has Constitution Day on Monday, December 6, and Spain, Italy and Portugal have the Immaculate Conception on Wednesday, December 8. Christmas is Saturday, December 25.

  1. Wed, Dec 1Public holiday

    Portugal's Restoration of Independence Day is on Wednesday, December 1.

  2. Mon, Dec 6Public holiday

    Constitution Day is a public holiday in Spain on Monday, December 6.

  3. Wed, Dec 8Public holiday

    Christmas Day falls on a Saturday in all four countries.

  5. Sun, Dec 26Regional or by country

    St Stephen's Day is a public holiday in Italy, and Greece observes the Synaxis of the Mother of God, on Sunday, December 26.

Frequently asked questions

When is Easter in Spain, Italy and Portugal compared with Greece?

Spain, Italy and Portugal celebrate Western Easter on Sunday, March 28, with Good Friday on March 26. Greece celebrates Orthodox Easter on Sunday, May 2, with Good Friday on April 30 and Easter Monday on May 3.

Is Good Friday a public holiday in Italy?

No. Good Friday is not a national holiday in Italy, only locally in South Tyrol. Easter Monday is the national Easter holiday. Good Friday is a national holiday in Spain and a public holiday in Portugal.

What is new about Italian holidays?

The Feast of St Francis of Assisi on October 4 is now a national holiday, and in 2027 it falls on Monday, giving a three-day weekend.

How many public holidays does Spain have in 2027?

Spain has 10 national holidays, 8 of which communities cannot replace. Each community adds its own and municipalities add two local days, so people can have up to 14 paid holidays.

Which holidays fall on weekends in 2027?

Liberation Day and Freedom Day (April 25), Labour Day (May 1), Assumption (August 15) and Christmas Day (December 25) all land on a weekend. Spanish communities decide whether to move some Sunday holidays, and the other countries mostly do not.

When do the clocks change in 2027?

Clocks go forward on Sunday, March 28 and back on Sunday, October 31 in all four countries.

Which holidays are not covered here?

Malta, Cyprus and local municipality holidays are not listed. Spanish autonomous community and Italian patron-saint days vary by place.

Is this an official list?

No. It combines government publications and public holiday calendars, listed under each month. Greek, Spanish and Portuguese dates for substitute days can change, so confirm with your employer or the official gazette. This page is informational only.

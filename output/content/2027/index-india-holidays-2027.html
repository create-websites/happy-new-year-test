January 2027

Pongal, Sankranti and a Republic Day long weekend

January has one central gazetted holiday, Republic Day on Tuesday, January 26, plus the harvest festivals of Makar Sankranti and Pongal.

  1. Fri, Jan 1State & regional

    New Year's Day is a regional holiday in several states, not a central gazetted holiday.

  2. Sat, Jan 2State & regional

    Mannam Jayanthi, a Kerala regional holiday, falls on Saturday, January 2.

  3. Jan 14-15Harvest & new year

    Makar Sankranti and Pongal mark the harvest season, especially in southern states such as Tamil Nadu. Calendars place them on January 14 or 15, so confirm your state's notified date.

  4. Tue, Jan 26Central gazetted holiday

    Republic Day is a fixed national holiday on a Tuesday. Taking Monday, January 25, off gives four days, from Saturday, January 23 to Tuesday, January 26.

February 2027

No central gazetted holidays, and Ramadan begins

February has no central gazetted holiday. The month is notable for the start of Ramadan, which depends on moon sighting.

  1. All monthPlan your leave

    The DoPT list has no gazetted holiday in February 2027, so plan leave and travel without holiday adjustments.

  2. About Feb 8Date may shift

    Ramadan is expected to begin around February 7-8, depending on moon sighting, and runs to about March 8.

March 2027

Eid, Holi and Good Friday make the best leave month

March packs Maha Shivaratri, Id-ul-Fitr, Holi and Good Friday. With three days of leave you can build a nine-day break from March 20 to 28.

  1. Sat, Mar 6Key festival

    Maha Shivaratri falls on Saturday, March 6. It is a restricted holiday for central offices and is a state holiday in many states.

  2. Wed, Mar 10Date may shift

    Id-ul-Fitr is a gazetted holiday on Wednesday, March 10. The date depends on moon sighting and can shift by a day. Taking March 11 and 12 off gives five days, March 10-14.

  3. Tue, Mar 23Central gazetted holiday

    Holi is a gazetted holiday on Tuesday, March 23 in the central list. Some panchangs place Holi on Monday, March 22, so check the notice for your state and employer.

  4. Fri, Mar 26Central gazetted holiday

    Good Friday is a gazetted holiday on March 26. Holy Thursday is March 25.

  5. Sun, Mar 28Key festival

    Easter Sunday falls on March 28.

  6. Mon, Mar 22Plan your leave

    Leave idea: taking Monday, March 22, and Wednesday and Thursday, March 24-25, links Holi and Good Friday into nine days off, from Saturday, March 20, to Sunday, March 28, for three days of leave.

April 2027

New years across India, then Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti

April is the month of regional new years: Ugadi, Gudi Padwa and Cheiraoba on April 7, and Baisakhi, Tamil Puthandu, Vishu and Bohag Bihu around April 14-15.

  1. Wed, Apr 7Harvest & new year

    Ugadi, Gudi Padwa and Cheiraoba mark new years in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Manipur, among others. Most calendars place them on Wednesday, April 7.

  2. Wed, Apr 14State & regional

    Baisakhi (Punjab), Tamil Puthandu, Vishu (Kerala), Bohag or Rongali Bihu (Assam) and the Bengali new year fall on April 14-15. Ambedkar Jayanti is also on April 14.

  3. Thu, Apr 15Central gazetted holiday

    Ram Navami is a gazetted holiday on Thursday, April 15.

  4. Mon, Apr 19Central gazetted holiday

    Mahavir Jayanti is a gazetted holiday on Monday, April 19. Taking Friday, April 16, off gives five days, April 15-19.

August 2027

Independence Day on a Sunday, then Janmashtami

  1. Sun, Aug 15Central gazetted holiday

October 2027

Navratri, Dussehra and a Friday Diwali

Navratri and Durga Puja build up to Dussehra on Saturday, October 9, and Diwali arrives on Friday, October 29, giving a three-day weekend.

  1. Sat, Oct 2Central gazetted holiday

    Mahatma Gandhi's birthday is a national holiday on Saturday, October 2.

  2. Oct 5-9Key festival

    Durga Puja festivities begin around October 5 and build up to Vijayadashami on October 9. Navratri begins around September 30. State holidays for Durga Puja differ, with the main observance in West Bengal, Odisha and Assam.

  3. Sat, Oct 9Central gazetted holiday

    Dussehra (Vijaya Dashmi) is a gazetted holiday on Saturday, October 9, so there is no extra day off.

  4. Mon, Oct 18Key festival

    Karva Chauth falls on Monday, October 18 and is a restricted holiday for central offices.

  5. Fri, Oct 29Central gazetted holiday

    Diwali (Deepavali) is a gazetted holiday on Friday, October 29, giving a three-day weekend, October 29-31. Some states add extra days around it.

Frequently asked questions

How many central government holidays are there in India in 2027?

The Department of Personnel and Training notified 17 gazetted holidays for Central Government offices in Delhi and New Delhi, in an office memorandum dated July 16, 2026. Employees can also choose two holidays from a list of 32 restricted holidays. Five of the dates fall on a Saturday or Sunday, and no substitute day is given.

Is the holiday list the same in every state?

No. India has no uniform holiday calendar. Central offices outside Delhi observe 14 compulsory holidays and three chosen locally, and each state government adds its own, such as Onam in Kerala, Chhath Puja in Bihar and Baisakhi in Punjab. Check your state's notification.

When are Holi, Dussehra and Diwali in 2027?

The central list gives Holi on Tuesday, March 23, although some panchangs place it on Monday, March 22. Dussehra is Saturday, October 9, and Diwali is Friday, October 29.

Which holiday dates can change?

Id-ul-Fitr (March 10), Id-ul-Zuha (May 17), Muharram (June 16) and Id-e-Milad (August 15) depend on moon sighting and can move by a day at short notice. Holi and Janmashtami are also shown on different days by some calendars.

When are banks closed in 2027?

Banks are closed on all Sundays, on the second and fourth Saturdays of each month, and on national and state holidays notified under the RBI's state-wise calendar. In the second half of 2027 the second and fourth Saturdays are June 12 and 26, July 10 and 24, August 14 and 28, September 11 and 25, October 9 and 23, November 13 and 27 and December 11 and 25.

What are the stock market holidays in 2027?

As of November 16, 2026, NSE and BSE had not released their official 2027 trading calendar. One published tentative list includes January 26, March 10, March 22 (Holi), March 26, April 15, April 19 and May 17, so treat it as indicative until the exchanges confirm.

Which festivals are state holidays rather than national ones?

Examples include Pongal and Tamil Puthandu in Tamil Nadu, Onam and Vishu in Kerala, Ugadi in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka, Gudi Padwa and Maharashtra Day in Maharashtra, Baisakhi in Punjab, Bohag Bihu in Assam and Durga Puja in West Bengal. See the state-by-state cards above.

Is this the official holiday list, and does it cover every state?

No. It is built from the DoPT central list and public calendars. It does not list every state or local holiday, and your employer, school or state government may differ. Verify with the official notification before booking travel. This page is informational only.

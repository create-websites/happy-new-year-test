March 2027

Eid al-Fitr, the first big break of the year

Eid al-Fitr falls around March 9-12 across the region, depending on moon sighting. The UAE could have a nine-day break with one leave day.

  1. Mar 9-12Moon-sighting dates

    Eid al-Fitr is expected around March 9-12. The UAE gives three days from March 9 to 11, extended to four if Ramadan lasts 30 days. Saudi Arabia lists March 9-12, Türkiye March 9-11 or 10-12, Qatar and Egypt March 10-12.

  2. Mon, Mar 8Plan your leave

    UAE leave idea: if the holiday runs March 9-12, taking Monday, March 8, off gives nine days off, March 6-14.

  3. Tue, Mar 9Work & weekends

    In Türkiye, the eve of the Bayram is a half-day, and the holiday itself lasts three days.

May 2027

Arafat Day and Eid al-Adha, plus Sham El Nessim

Arafat Day is May 15 and Eid al-Adha runs from May 16 to 18, with Türkiye observing four days. Egypt also has Labour Day and Sham El Nessim on May 3.

  1. Sat, May 1By country

    Labour Day falls on a Saturday. It is a public holiday in Egypt and Türkiye.

  2. Mon, May 3By country

    Sham El Nessim, the Egyptian spring festival, is observed on Monday, May 3.

  3. Sat, May 15Eid & Islamic holidays

    Arafat Day (Arafah) falls on Saturday, May 15 in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, subject to moon sighting. In Türkiye, May 15 is the eve of Kurban Bayramı and a half-day.

  4. May 16-18Moon-sighting dates

    Eid al-Adha runs from Sunday, May 16, to Tuesday, May 18, in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, a four-day break with Arafat Day. Türkiye observes Kurban Bayramı on May 16-19, and May 19 is also Atatürk Commemoration, Youth and Sports Day.

Frequently asked questions

When is Eid al-Fitr in 2027?

Eid al-Fitr is expected around March 9-12, depending on moon sighting. The UAE lists March 9-11, or up to March 12 if Ramadan lasts 30 days. Türkiye's Ramazan Bayramı is March 9-11 or 10-12 depending on the source.

When is Eid al-Adha in 2027?

Arafat Day is expected on Saturday, May 15 and Eid al-Adha from Sunday, May 16 to Tuesday, May 18 in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Türkiye's Kurban Bayramı runs May 16-19.

How many public holidays does the UAE have in 2027?

The UAE has seven holiday occasions: New Year's Day, Eid al-Fitr, Arafat Day with Eid al-Adha, Hijri New Year, the Prophet's Birthday and Eid Al Etihad. The number of days depends on how long Ramadan lasts.

How many public holidays does Saudi Arabia have?

Saudi Arabia observes four public holidays: Founding Day on February 22, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and National Day on September 23.

Are weekends the same across these countries?

No. The UAE and Türkiye have Saturday and Sunday weekends. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt have Friday and Saturday weekends, which changes how a holiday links to the weekend.

Which dates can change?

Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Arafat Day, the Hijri New Year and the Prophet's Birthday depend on moon sighting and can shift by a day or two. The UAE and Saudi Arabia confirm them shortly before the date.

Which holidays are not covered here?

Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, Iran, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Morocco and Egypt's June 30 and July 23 revolution holidays are not listed.

Is this an official list?

No. It combines government announcements where available with public holiday calendars, listed under each month. Islamic dates are expected dates and may change. Confirm with your employer or the official announcement. This page is informational only.

January 2027

New Year's Day and Thaipusam

New Year's Day is Friday, January 1, in all four countries. Indonesia has Isra Mikraj on Tuesday, January 5, and Malaysia has Thaipusam in several states on January 22.

  1. Fri, Jan 1National holiday

    New Year's Day is a public holiday in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand, giving a three-day weekend.

  2. Tue, Jan 5Religious holiday

    Isra Mikraj is a national holiday in Indonesia on Tuesday, January 5.

  3. Fri, Jan 22Festival

    Thaipusam is a public holiday on Friday, January 22, in Kuala Lumpur, Negeri Sembilan, Perak, Penang, Putrajaya and Selangor.

February 2027

Chinese New Year and Thailand's Magha Puja

Chinese New Year falls on Saturday and Sunday, February 6-7. Singapore adds Monday, February 8, and Indonesia has a shared leave day on February 5. Thailand's Magha Puja substitute is Monday, February 22.

  1. Mon, Feb 1By country

    Federal Territory Day is a holiday on Monday, February 1, in Kuala Lumpur, Labuan and Putrajaya.

  2. Feb 5Substitute or in-lieu day

    Indonesia has a cuti bersama (shared leave day) on Friday, February 5, ahead of Chinese New Year.

  3. Feb 6-7Festival

    Chinese New Year falls on Saturday and Sunday, February 6-7, in Singapore and Malaysia. Indonesia lists Imlek on February 6.

  4. Mon, Feb 8Substitute or in-lieu day

    Singapore adds Monday, February 8, as an additional public holiday for Chinese New Year, per the Ministry of Manpower.

  5. Mon, Feb 22Substitute or in-lieu day

    Magha Puja Day falls on Sunday, February 21, so Monday, February 22, is the substitute day in Thailand's bank holiday list.

March 2027

Hari Raya Puasa, Nyepi and Easter

Hari Raya Puasa is March 10 in Singapore and Malaysia, with Idulfitri on March 10-11 in Indonesia, supported by four shared leave days. Good Friday is March 26.

  1. Mon, Mar 8Religious holiday

    Nyepi (Balinese Day of Silence) is a national holiday in Indonesia on Monday, March 8.

  2. Mar 9-15Religious holiday

    Indonesia's Idulfitri holiday is March 10-11, with shared leave days on March 9, 12 and 15.

  3. Wed, Mar 10Religious holiday

    Hari Raya Puasa is March 10 in Singapore and Malaysia. Malaysia also has a second day on March 11. Dates are subject to change.

  4. Thu, Mar 25Religious holiday

    Indonesia has a shared leave day on Thursday, March 25, before Good Friday.

  5. Fri, Mar 26Religious holiday

    Good Friday is a holiday in Singapore and Indonesia, and in Sabah and Sarawak in Malaysia.

  6. Sun, Mar 28Religious holiday

    Easter Sunday is a national holiday in Indonesia.

May 2027

Labour Day, Hari Raya Haji and Vesak

Labour Day falls on Saturday, May 1. Hari Raya Haji is May 17, and Vesak or Waisak is May 20. Indonesia adds shared leave days on May 18 and 19.

  1. Sat, May 1Substitute or in-lieu day

    Labour Day falls on a Saturday in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia. Thailand moves its bank holiday to Monday, May 3.

  2. Tue, May 4National holiday

    Coronation Day is a public holiday in Thailand on Tuesday, May 4.

  3. Thu, May 6Religious holiday

    The Ascension of Jesus Christ is a national holiday in Indonesia on Thursday, May 6.

  4. Mon, May 17Religious holiday

    Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha) falls on Monday, May 17, in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia. Malaysia adds May 18 in Kelantan and Terengganu.

  5. May 18-19Substitute or in-lieu day

    Indonesia has shared leave days on Tuesday, May 18, and Wednesday, May 19, giving a long break with Waisak.

  6. Thu, May 20Festival

    Vesak Day (Wesak, Waisak, Visakha Bucha) falls on Thursday, May 20, in all four countries.

  7. May 30-31Festival

    Kaamatan Festival is a holiday in Sabah and Labuan on May 30-31.

June 2027

Pancasila Day, Hijri New Year and Gawai

Indonesia's Pancasila Day is Tuesday, June 1, and the Islamic New Year is Sunday, June 6. Malaysia marks the King's official birthday on Monday, June 7.

  1. Tue, Jun 1National holiday

    Pancasila Day is a national holiday in Indonesia on Tuesday, June 1. Gawai Dayak runs June 1-2 in Sarawak.

  2. Thu, Jun 3National holiday

    Queen Suthida's Birthday is a public holiday in Thailand on Thursday, June 3.

  3. Sun, Jun 6Religious holiday

    The Islamic New Year (Awal Muharram, 1 Muharam) falls on Sunday, June 6, in Malaysia and Indonesia.

  4. Mon, Jun 7National holiday

    The official birthday of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong is a public holiday in Malaysia on Monday, June 7.

Deepavali and Thai remembrance days

Deepavali falls on Thursday, October 28, in Singapore and Malaysia (except Sarawak). Thailand marks King Bhumibol Memorial Day on October 13 and Chulalongkorn Day on October 25.

December 2027

Christmas and the Thai year-end

Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25. Thailand's King Bhumibol Day substitute is Monday, December 6. Indonesia adds Isra Mikraj on December 26.

  1. Mon, Dec 6Substitute or in-lieu day

    King Bhumibol's Birthday, National Day and Father's Day falls on Sunday, December 5, so Monday, December 6, is the substitute day in Thailand.

  2. Fri, Dec 10National holiday

    Constitution Day is a public holiday in Thailand on Friday, December 10.

  3. Fri, Dec 24Substitute or in-lieu day

    Indonesia has a shared leave day on Friday, December 24. Sabah observes Christmas Eve.

  4. Sat, Dec 25Festival

    Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25, in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.

  5. Sun, Dec 26Religious holiday

    Isra Mikraj (1449 H) is a second national holiday of the year in Indonesia on Sunday, December 26.

  6. Fri, Dec 31Work & business

    New Year's Eve is a bank holiday in Thailand on Friday, December 31.

Frequently asked questions

How many public holidays does Singapore have in 2027?

Singapore has 11 gazetted public holidays in 2027, plus an additional day on Monday, February 8, announced by the Ministry of Manpower for Chinese New Year.

What are the main Malaysian public holidays in 2027?

Malaysia lists 15 nationwide holidays, including Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Wesak Day, National Day on August 31 and Malaysia Day on September 16. Some states add their own days.

How many holidays does Indonesia have in 2027?

Indonesia has 18 national holidays and 8 cuti bersama (shared leave days) in 2027. The decree was signed on September 15, 2026.

When is Songkran in 2027?

Songkran runs from Tuesday, April 13, to Thursday, April 15, 2027, according to the Bank of Thailand's holiday list.

What is a cuti bersama in Indonesia?

A cuti bersama is a shared leave day set by the government around national holidays. For private employees it counts as part of annual leave and its use is optional.

Which dates can change?

Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji, the Islamic New Year, the Prophet's Birthday and Deepavali in Malaysia are marked subject to change. Confirm closer to the date.

Which countries are not covered here?

The Philippines, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Brunei and Timor-Leste are not listed. Thailand's list is the Bank of Thailand's, which can differ from the government and private-sector lists.

Is this an official list?

No. It combines government announcements where available with published calendars, listed under each month. Confirm with your employer or the official source. This page is informational only.

January 2027

New Year on a Friday, and Scotland's extra Monday

New Year's Day falls on Friday, January 1. Scotland's 2 January holiday lands on a Saturday, so Scotland takes a substitute day on Monday, January 4.

  1. Fri, Jan 1Bank holiday

    New Year's Day is a bank holiday in all four nations.

  2. Mon, Jan 4Substitute or varies

    Scotland observes a substitute day for 2 January on Monday, January 4. England, Wales and Northern Ireland have no extra day.

  3. Jan 5-8Plan your leave

    Leave idea for Scotland: four days of leave on January 5-8 turn the New Year holidays into a ten-day break, January 1-10.

  4. Mon, Jan 25Tradition & faith

    Burns Night is celebrated across Scotland on January 25. It is a tradition, not a bank holiday.

February 2027

No bank holidays, but Pancake Day and Lent

February has no bank holidays in any nation. Chinese New Year, Pancake Day and the start of Lent fall in the month, and Ramadan is expected to begin around February 8.

  1. All monthPlan your leave

    No bank holiday falls in February 2027 in any UK nation.

  2. Sat, Feb 6Tradition & faith

    Chinese New Year falls on Saturday, February 6.

  3. Tue, Feb 9Tradition & faith

    Shrove Tuesday (Pancake Day) is on Tuesday, February 9, and Ash Wednesday, the start of Lent, follows on February 10. Both are set by Easter.

  4. Sun, Feb 14Tradition & faith

    Valentine's Day falls on Sunday, February 14.

  5. About Feb 8Substitute or varies

    Ramadan is expected to begin around February 7-8 depending on moon sighting, and runs to about March 8.

March 2027

Easter arrives early and the clocks go forward

Good Friday is March 26 and Easter Monday March 29. Northern Ireland also has St Patrick's Day on March 17, and Scotland has no Easter Monday holiday.

  1. Sun, Mar 7Tradition & faith

    Mothering Sunday falls on Sunday, March 7, three weeks before Easter.

  2. Wed, Mar 10Substitute or varies

    Eid al-Fitr is expected around Wednesday, March 10, depending on moon sighting. It is not a bank holiday.

  3. Wed, Mar 17Nation-specific

    St Patrick's Day is a bank holiday in Northern Ireland only, on Wednesday, March 17.

  4. Fri, Mar 26Bank holiday

    Good Friday is a bank holiday in all four nations.

  5. Sun, Mar 28Clocks & seasons

    Easter Sunday and the start of British Summer Time fall on the same day, March 28: clocks go forward one hour at 1am.

  6. Mon, Mar 29Substitute or varies

    Easter Monday is a bank holiday in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, but not in Scotland.

  7. Mar 30-Apr 2Plan your leave

    Leave idea: four days of leave on March 30 to April 2 turn Easter into a ten-day break, March 26 to April 4.

May 2027

Two Monday bank holidays in every nation

The Early May bank holiday is Monday, May 3, and the Spring bank holiday is Monday, May 31. Both apply across the whole UK and each makes a three-day weekend.

  1. Mon, May 3Bank holiday

    The Early May bank holiday falls on Monday, May 3 in all four nations, giving a three-day weekend, May 1-3.

  2. Mon, May 17Substitute or varies

    Eid al-Adha is expected around Monday, May 17, depending on moon sighting. It is not a bank holiday.

  3. Mon, May 31Bank holiday

    The Spring bank holiday falls on Monday, May 31 in all four nations. The London Stock Exchange lists it as a non-trading day.

  4. Book a dayPlan your leave

    A single day of leave next to either May bank holiday turns the three-day weekend into a four-day break.

Frequently asked questions

How many bank holidays are there in the UK in 2027?

England and Wales have 8, Scotland has 9 and Northern Ireland has 10, according to the GOV.UK lists as reproduced by several travel and HR sites. Christmas Day and Boxing Day both fall on a weekend, so they move to Monday, December 27 and Tuesday, December 28.

When are Good Friday and Easter Monday in 2027?

Good Friday is March 26 and Easter Monday is March 29, with Easter Sunday on March 28. Easter Monday is a bank holiday in England, Wales and Northern Ireland but not in Scotland.

Which holidays differ between the UK nations?

Scotland has a substitute day on January 4 for 2 January, a summer holiday on August 2 and St Andrew's Day on November 30. Northern Ireland has St Patrick's Day on March 17 and the Battle of the Boyne on July 12. England and Wales have neither.

What are the two May bank holidays in 2027?

The Early May bank holiday is Monday, May 3 and the Spring bank holiday is Monday, May 31. Both apply in all three bank holiday regions.

Do I get paid for a bank holiday?

Not automatically. GOV.UK says employers do not have to give paid leave on bank or public holidays, so it depends on your contract. Some contracts count bank holidays inside the annual leave allowance.

Is the London Stock Exchange open on bank holidays?

The exchange recognises England and Wales bank holidays, and lists Monday, May 31, 2027 as a non-trading day. Calendar sites also report early closes around Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, so check the exchange's business days page before trading.

When do the clocks change in 2027?

Clocks go forward one hour on Sunday, March 28 and back one hour on Sunday, October 31. The spring change falls on Easter Sunday.

Is this the official bank holiday list?

No. It follows the GOV.UK dates as reproduced by public sources, and adds festivals and leave ideas. Some local council holidays in Scotland are not listed, and your contract or school may differ. Confirm with GOV.UK or your employer. This page is informational only.

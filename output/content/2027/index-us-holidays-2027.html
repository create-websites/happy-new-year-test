January 2027

A Friday New Year and an MLK long weekend

The year opens with two three-day weekends: New Year's Day on Friday, January 1, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day on Monday, January 18.

  1. Fri, Jan 1Federal holiday

    New Year's Day is a federal holiday, and the NYSE and Nasdaq are closed. It falls on a Friday, so it makes a three-day weekend (January 1-3).

  2. Thu, Jan 7Faith & culture

    Eastern Orthodox Christmas is observed on January 7.

  3. Fri, Jan 15Faith & culture

    A Hindu harvest festival (Makar Sankranti) falls on January 15, with feasting and kite flying.

  4. Mon, Jan 18Federal holiday

    Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr. is observed on the third Monday of January. Federal offices and the stock markets are closed, giving a long weekend of January 16-18.

February 2027

Lunar New Year, Ramadan, Lent and a Super Bowl Valentine's Sunday

February is crowded with observances: Lunar New Year, the start of Ramadan, Ash Wednesday and Valentine's Day, plus the Presidents' Day long weekend.

  1. Sat, Feb 6Faith & culture

    Lunar New Year falls on Saturday, February 6, celebrated across Asia and by communities worldwide.

  2. Sun, Feb 7Faith & culture

    Ramadan begins at sundown on Sunday, February 7, and runs to sunset on Monday, March 8.

  3. Wed, Feb 10Faith & culture

    Ash Wednesday begins Lent, which runs through March 25.

  4. Sun, Feb 14Family & fun

    Valentine's Day falls on a Sunday. One calendar also lists Super Bowl Sunday on February 14.

  5. Mon, Feb 15Federal holiday

    Washington's Birthday, widely called Presidents' Day, is the third Monday of February. Federal offices and the stock markets are closed, for a long weekend of February 13-15.

March 2027

Clocks spring forward and Easter arrives early

Daylight saving time starts on March 14, Eid al-Fitr and Holi bring celebrations, and Good Friday (a market holiday) leads into an early Easter on March 28.

  1. Sat, Mar 6Faith & culture

    Maha Shivaratri falls on Saturday, March 6. Laylat al-Qadr (Night of Power) is observed around March 5-6.

  2. Tue, Mar 9Faith & culture

    Eid al-Fitr, the end of Ramadan, is expected on March 9-10. The exact day depends on moon sighting and can vary by a day.

  3. Sun, Mar 14Clocks & seasons

    Daylight saving time starts, by federal law on the second Sunday of March. Clocks move forward an hour.

  4. Wed, Mar 17Family & fun

    St. Patrick's Day falls on a Wednesday.

  5. Sat, Mar 20Faith & culture

    Nowruz, the Persian and Baha'i new year, falls on March 20 or 21 depending on the source.

  6. Mon, Mar 22Faith & culture

    Holi, the Hindu festival of colors, is on Monday, March 22, and Purim is observed March 22-23.

  7. Fri, Mar 26Markets & banks

    Good Friday is not a federal holiday, but the NYSE and Nasdaq are closed. Holy Thursday is March 25.

  8. Sun, Mar 28Faith & culture

    Easter Sunday is on March 28, an early date in the possible range.

June 2027

Juneteenth moves to Friday, and Father's Day closes the weekend

Juneteenth falls on a Saturday, so federal employees get Friday, June 18 off and the stock markets close that day. Father's Day follows on Sunday.

  1. Mon, Jun 14Family & fun

    Flag Day, June 14, is an observance and not a day off.

  2. Fri, Jun 18Observed on another day

    Juneteenth is observed on Friday because June 19 is a Saturday. Federal employees are off, the NYSE and Nasdaq are closed, and the long weekend runs June 18-20.

  3. Sat, Jun 19Federal holiday

    Juneteenth National Independence Day itself falls on Saturday, June 19.

  4. Sun, Jun 20Family & fun

    Father's Day is the third Sunday of June.

July 2027

Independence Day on a Sunday, observed Monday

November 2027

Clocks fall back, Veterans Day and a four-day Thanksgiving

Election Day, the end of daylight saving time, Veterans Day and Thanksgiving make November the busiest month for the calendar after December.

  1. Tue, Nov 2Federal holiday

    Election Day is Tuesday, November 2. It is not a federal general-election year and not a federal holiday, but some states observe it.

  2. Sun, Nov 7Clocks & seasons

    Daylight saving time ends on the first Sunday of November. Clocks move back an hour.

  3. Thu, Nov 11Federal holiday

    Veterans Day is a federal holiday on Thursday, November 11. The NYSE and Nasdaq are open.

  4. Thu, Nov 25Federal holiday

    Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November. Federal offices and the stock markets are closed.

  5. Fri, Nov 26Markets & banks

    The day after Thanksgiving (Black Friday) is not a federal holiday. Markets close early at 1:00 p.m. ET, the only early close of 2027.

  6. Mon, Nov 22Plan your leave

    Leave idea: taking Monday to Wednesday and Friday, November 22-24 and 26, off makes nine days off, from Saturday, November 20, to Sunday, November 28, for four days of leave.

December 2027

Christmas observed on Friday, and a bonus day on December 31

Christmas Day is a Saturday, so the holiday moves to Friday, December 24. New Year's Day 2028 is also a Saturday, so federal employees get Friday, December 31 off.

  1. Fri, Dec 24Observed on another day

    Christmas is observed on Friday, December 24 for federal employees, and the NYSE and Nasdaq are closed. There is no early close because the full day is a holiday.

  2. Sat, Dec 25Federal holiday

    Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25.

  3. Fri, Dec 31Observed on another day

    Federal employees are off on Friday, December 31, which is the observed date for New Year's Day 2028 (a Saturday). Private employers and markets set their own schedules.

  4. Mon, Dec 27Plan your leave

    Leave idea: taking Monday to Thursday, December 27-30, off turns the two bonus Fridays into ten days off, from December 24 to January 2, for four days of leave.

Frequently asked questions

How many federal holidays are there in 2027?

There are 11 federal holidays in 2027. Three fall on a weekend and are observed on a weekday: Juneteenth on Friday, June 18, Independence Day on Monday, July 5, and Christmas on Friday, December 24. Federal employees also get Friday, December 31 off because New Year's Day 2028 is a Saturday.

What are the long weekends in 2027?

There are eleven: New Year's Day (January 1-3), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 16-18), Presidents' Day (February 13-15), Memorial Day (May 29-31), Juneteenth (June 18-20), Independence Day (July 3-5), Labor Day (September 4-6), Columbus Day (October 9-11), Thanksgiving (November 25-28), Christmas (December 24-26) and the end of year (December 31 to January 2).

Is the stock market closed on federal holidays in 2027?

Mostly. The NYSE and Nasdaq close on ten weekdays in 2027: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday (March 26), Memorial Day, Juneteenth (June 18), Independence Day (July 5), Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas (December 24). They stay open on Columbus Day and Veterans Day, and close early at 1:00 p.m. ET on Friday, November 26.

When are Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day and Thanksgiving in 2027?

Easter is Sunday, March 28, Mother's Day is Sunday, May 9, Father's Day is Sunday, June 20 and Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25.

When do the clocks change in 2027?

Daylight saving time starts on Sunday, March 14, and ends on Sunday, November 7.

Do private employers have to give federal holidays off?

No. Federal holidays are set for federal employees, and most private businesses, banks and markets observe them but are not legally required to. Check your employer's policy and your state's holidays.

When are Eid, Diwali and Lunar New Year in 2027?

Lunar New Year is Saturday, February 6. Ramadan begins at sundown on February 7, and Eid al-Fitr is expected on March 9-10 and Eid al-Adha on May 16-17, depending on moon sighting. Diwali is Friday, October 29.

Does this calendar include state holidays or give legal advice?

No. It covers federal holidays, stock market closures and popular observances. State and local holidays, school calendars and employer policies differ, so confirm with the relevant office. This page is informational and not legal or financial advice.

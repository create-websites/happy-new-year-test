Labour Day falls on Monday, October 4, in New South Wales, the ACT and South Australia. Queensland holds its King's Birthday on the same day. New Zealand's Labour Day is Monday, October 25.

  1. Mon, Oct 4National or state holiday

    Labour Day is a public holiday in NSW, the ACT and South Australia on Monday, October 4. Queensland's King's Birthday falls on the same day.

  2. Thu, Oct 21By state or territory

    Royal Hobart Show Day is a holiday in Hobart and some other Tasmanian areas on Thursday, October 21.

  3. Mon, Oct 25National or state holiday

    Labour Day is a public holiday in New Zealand on Monday, October 25.

November 2027

Melbourne Cup and Recreation Day

Melbourne Cup Day is Tuesday, November 2, in Victoria. Tasmania's Recreation Day is Monday, November 1, in areas that do not observe the Regatta.

  1. Mon, Nov 1By state or territory

    Recreation Day is a holiday in parts of Tasmania on Monday, November 1.

  2. Tue, Nov 2Local or cultural day

    Melbourne Cup Day is a public holiday in Victoria on Tuesday, November 2, though some regional areas hold a local alternative.

December 2027

Christmas on a Saturday

Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25, so most places have Monday, December 27, and Tuesday, December 28, as the additional days. Tasmania's Boxing Day is Tuesday, December 28.

  1. Fri, Dec 24By state or territory

    Christmas Eve is a part-day holiday (from 6 or 7 pm) in Queensland, South Australia and the Northern Territory.

  2. Mon, Dec 27Substitute or additional day

    The additional Christmas Day holiday falls on Monday, December 27, in Australia and New Zealand.

  3. Tue, Dec 28Substitute or additional day

    The additional Boxing Day holiday falls on Tuesday, December 28, in most places. South Australia observes a Proclamation Day holiday.

  4. Fri, Dec 31Work & business

    New Year's Eve is a part-day holiday (from 7 pm) in South Australia and the Northern Territory.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see the holidays for my country?

Pick a region in the selector, then choose a country or state inside it, a month, or type a holiday name in the search box. The table and the month-by-month list both update.

Which regions does this page cover?

It combines ten regional 2027 calendars: the United States, India, the United Kingdom, Western and Central Europe, Southern Europe, the Nordic countries, the Middle East and Türkiye, China, Japan and South Korea, Southeast Asia, and Australia and New Zealand.

Which holiday dates can still change?

Dates tied to the moon or to local sighting, such as Eid, Hari Raya, the Islamic New Year and some Hindu festivals, can move by a day or two. Some governments also add or move days during the year. Each region page marks the dates that are approximate.

Do weekends differ between countries?

Yes. The UAE and Türkiye take Saturday and Sunday off, while Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt take Friday and Saturday, so the same holiday links to the weekend differently. The Middle East entry explains this.

Why do some holidays move to a Monday?

Several countries add a substitute day when a holiday falls on a weekend, for example South Korea, Thailand, the UK, Australia and New Zealand. The Break column shows the resulting long weekend.

Which places are not covered yet?

Canada, Mexico, Brazil and the rest of Latin America, Africa, Central and Eastern Europe, the Philippines, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Taiwan and several Middle Eastern countries are not covered yet.

Is this an official list?

No. Dates come from government announcements where available and from published public holiday calendars, listed under each month. Confirm with your employer or the official source before booking travel. This page is informational only.

September 4, 2027

Ganesh Chaturthi: Begin with Wisdom & Joy

Start September with Lord Ganesha: home sthapana, puja, modaks, family gatherings, community pandals and thoughtful eco-friendly celebration. The festival also opens a busy stretch of autumn observances.

🌿 Eco-friendly Ganesha

Choose natural clay, reusable decor and responsible visarjan practices.

🍬 Modak & Prasad

Plan family cooking, offerings and festive sweets before puja day.

🪔 Home Puja

Prepare flowers, durva, lamps, fruit and a clean, welcoming puja space.

🥁 Community Celebration

Visit local pandals, cultural programs and visarjan processions respectfully.

Explore Ganesh Chaturthi

September 12, 2027

Onam: Harvest, Homecoming & Togetherness

Celebrate Kerala’s harvest festival through pookalam, Onasadya, traditional dress, music, games and the stories associated with King Mahabali.

🌼

Pookalam

Create a floral rangoli with family members and let the design grow more elaborate through the festival season.

🍛

Onasadya

Plan the vegetarian feast, banana-leaf presentation and shared meal as the heart of the celebration.

🏡

Homecoming

Use Onam as a reason to reconnect with family, share stories and celebrate Malayalam culture across generations.

Explore Onam

September 16–29, 2027

Pitru Paksha: Pause, Remember & Honor

The mood of September changes after the harvest and Ganesh celebrations. Pitru Paksha is a period for remembering ancestors through Shraddh, tarpan, pind daan, charity, food offerings and family remembrance according to one’s tradition.

💧 Tarpan

Offer water and prayers with attention to family tradition and local priestly guidance.

🍚 Pind Daan & Food Offerings

Understand the symbolic role of food, charity and feeding others during remembrance rituals.

🕯️ Family Memory

Share stories, photographs and values associated with parents, grandparents and earlier generations.

Explore Pitru Paksha & Shraddh

Begins September 30, 2027

Navratri Opens the Autumn Celebration Run

September closes with Ghatasthapana and the beginning of Navratri. The nine nights lead directly into Durga Puja, Maha Navami and Dussehra: then October continues toward Karwa Chauth and Diwali.

🪔 Navratri

Nine nights of devotion, fasting, garba, dandiya and regional traditions.

🌺 Durga Puja

Pandal culture, worship, food, community and the culmination of Devi celebrations.

🏹 Dussehra : Oct 9

Vijayadashami traditions, Ramleela, Ravana effigies and victory-of-good themes.

🪔 Diwali : Oct 29

Start early on cleaning, gifts, diyas, rangoli, sweets and family plans.

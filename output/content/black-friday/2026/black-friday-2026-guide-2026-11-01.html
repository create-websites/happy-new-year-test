10 minutes of prep could save more

Five moves before you hit “buy”

Record the 30–90 day price

Use Google Shopping price insights or Keepa price history to look beyond a retailer's strike-through number.

Track exact size, color & SKU

Google's price alerts can include desired size, color and price. A look-alike model may be a different product.

Buy only below your ceiling

Set a limit before opening ads. If the item stays above it, wait or skip rather than rationalizing a weak discount.

Look at the all-in cost

Check tax, shipping, paid memberships, installation, accessories, subscriptions and auto-renewal before comparing offers.

Review the return policy

Confirm whether a discounted item is final sale, whether gifts can be returned in January, and who pays return postage.

Separate urgent needs from wants

If an item is genuinely needed, a verified great early price may be better than waiting. If it's a want, favor patience.

Protect your money

Do not let a fake deal beat you

  • Do not trust the ad alone. Search the seller name and site address alongside “complaint” or “scam,” especially after seeing a social-media ad.
  • Use a credit card when possible. It usually provides a dispute route when goods never arrive or aren't what was ordered.
  • Avoid gift-card, crypto or wire-only checkout. The FTC identifies these demands as major warning signs.
  • Save confirmations and receipts. Check the shipping date, model number, and actual return terms before placing the order.

Best Buy also explicitly excludes qualified competitor offers from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the following Monday, so do not assume competitor price matching will work on Black Friday. See Best Buy terms effective Sep 2, 2026.

Quick answers

Black Friday 2026 FAQ

The key answers to help you decide when to buy and how to compare prices.

When is Black Friday 2026?
Friday, November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30, but several retailers start their promotions in late October or early November.
Are the Costco prices above available today?
No. They are advertised future November 2026 offers, mostly for November 27–30 with some earlier start dates. Verify price, stock, item model and store conditions at checkout.
Do I have to wait until November 27 for the best deal?
No. Lowe's starts offers October 29; Target has early deals November 5–7; Best Buy starts early deals November 6; and Walmart's first major event is November 12–15. Buy early only when the exact product is below your pre-set price ceiling and its return policy is acceptable.
Which categories should I watch on Black Friday vs. Cyber Monday?
Adobe's September 28 forecast expects Black Friday to be strongest for TVs, toys, clothing, furniture and appliances; it expects electronics and computers to reach their deepest category discounts on Cyber Monday. Actual products may not follow the category-wide pattern.
How can I tell whether a 50% off sign is a real bargain?
Compare the current price with a typical recent selling price for the exact model, not only the crossed-out list price. Include delivery, tax, membership fees, accessories, subscriptions and the cost of returns.
What are thoughtful Black Friday gifts under $50?
Consider a photo-memory print set, a favorite book with a personal note, a small repair kit, local food treats, a simple STEM project or practical cozy gifts. Target's 2026 holiday assortment includes pajamas from $10 and selected cashmere items from $25, while supplies last.

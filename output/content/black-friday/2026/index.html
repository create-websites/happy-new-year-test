10 habits that protect your savings

The biggest percentage-off label isn't necessarily the best deal.

1. Price-history first

Compare the precise model with recent sale prices, rather than just the MSRP or the crossed-out list price.

2. Use a buy-now threshold

For an essential purchase, establish a maximum price limit before the buying frenzy begins.

3. Watch the variant

TV panel, laptop RAM/SSD, headphone package, and model year can subtly alter the value.

4. Calculate full cost

Shipping, sales tax, membership charges, installation, and warranties can eliminate any savings.

5. Don't chase store-door lotteries

Gift cards and prizes are available in limited quantities. Consider the value of your time, travel, and the potential for disappointment.

6. Watch return windows

Compare holiday return policies, final-sale exceptions, restocking charges, and gifts purchased in advance.

7. Don't assume price matches

Retailer policies include exclusions that apply during major sales events and to specific marketplace sellers.

8. Prefer official or established sellers

Exercise caution with unknown domains, fake listings, unusual payment requests, or sellers without any history.

9. Buy essentials in volume only if useful

Everyday essentials can be wise buys: unless they go to waste due to storage issues or expiration.

10. Recheck on Cyber Monday

Offers cycle. When qualified, take advantage of retailer price-drop guarantees instead of repurchasing on impulse.

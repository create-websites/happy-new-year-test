About this collection

This 50-part series is organized in five thematic waves using the supplied article briefs. Its illustrations are New Year 2027 greeting cards from the provided multilingual collection, not photographs documenting the locations. Places and activities are described for context; specific public events and access arrangements can change each year.

For the strongest travel experience, compare official event programs, transport arrangements and local cultural guidance close to your intended dates. We have also distinguished family folklore from organized public celebrations and corrected a few commonly repeated misconceptions.

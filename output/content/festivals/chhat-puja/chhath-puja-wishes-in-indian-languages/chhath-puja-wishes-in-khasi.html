Copy and share a Chhath greeting

Review note: Meghalaya is largely Christian and Niam Khasi, so these are friendly greetings to Bihari and Hindi-speaking friends in Shillong. Draft : please have a native speaker review before posting. English meanings are given below each wish.

  1. Khublei shibun ia ka Chhath Puja! Ban long ka jingkmen bad ka jingkyrkhu ha phi bad ki longiing jong phi. Warm greetings for Chhath Puja! May joy and blessings be with you and your family.
  2. Ban kyntiew ka jingshai jong ka Sngi ia ka jingim jong phi. May the light of the Sun lift up your life.
  3. Hynrei jlan ka miet ka long kaba dum, ka Sngi kan sa mih biang : kane ka Chhath ka hikai ia ka jingkyrmen. However dark the night, the Sun will rise again : Chhath teaches hope.
  4. Ia ki lok ki ba shong jngai na ka iing ha Shillong : ban kmen phi ha kane ka sngi. 🌅 To friends living far from home in Shillong : be joyful on this day.
  5. Ban long ka koit ka khiah bad ka jingkmen ia baroh. May there be good health and happiness for all.

Chhath Puja at home

The home-puja guide describes a temporary water setup, a position with morning and evening sunlight, and a sugarcane canopy decorated with flowers and diyas. A separate section covers cooking arrangements. The added safety note distinguishes this cultural description from approval for a balcony pool or cooking installation.

How families prepare

This preparation account moves from household and kitchen cleaning to washing and drying the wheat used for Thekua. It then describes shopping for baskets, stoves, wood and fruits, and setting aside a clean room. The article shows how the source presents Chhath as a coordinated household effort.

Planning a temporary ghat

The source offers a brick-and-tarpaulin method and an inflatable-pool method. It mentions clean water, a sugarcane canopy and floral decoration. Because it supplies no structural assessment, the page prominently separates those descriptions from a site-specific design and does not treat a terrace or balcony as automatically suitable.

Eco-friendly celebration

The sustainability article recommends bamboo baskets and earthen pots, discusses traditional lighting, and emphasizes removing wrappers and other personal waste after puja. It also mentions artificial ponds in urban settings. These are the source's suggestions; the page does not claim to verify a particular pond or a materials lifecycle comparison.

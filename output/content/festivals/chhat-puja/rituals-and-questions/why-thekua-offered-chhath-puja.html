Thekua: The Crown Jewel of Chhath Prasad

Thekua (also known as Khajuria) is a deep-fried sweet made from whole wheat flour, jaggery, and ghee. It is the most essential offering in the Chhath Soop.

Symbolism and Practicality

  • Harvest Offering: Chhath coincides with the post-monsoon harvest. Wheat and sugarcane (jaggery) are major regional crops, making Thekua a direct offering of the season's first fruits to the Sun God.
  • Longevity: Thekua naturally resists spoiling and has a long shelf life. Historically, this made it the perfect Prasad for travelers and a lasting symbol of the Vrati's enduring devotion.
  • Sun Symbolism: The round or leaf-like shape of Thekua, pressed onto wooden molds, visually mimics the shape of the sun and nature's bounty.

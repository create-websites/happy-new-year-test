Rangoli Designs Specifically for Naraka Chaturdashi

Article 32 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Lamps & home preparation/Article 32

A Naraka Chaturdashi rangoli can surround a lamp, illuminate an entrance, or serve as a communal artistic activity. There is no single mandatory design distinguishing it from Lakshmi Puja rangoli. Lotus motifs, dots, circles, and geometric borders are commonly found throughout festive home decorations.

Choose a design for the space

For a tight entrance, opt for a small circle surrounded by a ring of petals. A broader courtyard can hold a dot grid or a repeated border. Ensure there is ample clear floor space so people can enter without walking over the design.

Build from a simple outline

Identify the center, set the outer edge, and proceed from broad shapes to finer details. Using two or three colors often appears more harmonious than a busy array. Allow children to color within a defined area while an adult oversees the main outline.

Select materials thoughtfully

Rice flour, petals, and appropriate rangoli powders produce various effects. Take into account the floor surface, ease of cleaning, and who might come into contact with the materials. Refrain from using loose glitter near food or areas where it could disperse outside.

Add light without crowding the artwork

Position a lamp on an independent heat-resistant stand away from the walking path. Battery-operated lamps function effectively within a detailed setup. Capture a top-down photo of the completed design before the evening rush, and document the pattern for future adjustments next year.

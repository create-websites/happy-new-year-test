The Glowing Skin Diet for Roop Chaturdashi

Article 18 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Bathing & renewal/Article 18

A festive eating routine can help ensure a comfortable day without guaranteeing a radiant complexion. Choti Diwali usually occurs during busy shopping, cooking, and late nights, so the best strategy is to have regular meals and allow sufficient time to enjoy them.

Build meals from familiar foods

A typical plate could consist of dal, vegetables, rice or roti, along with fruit or curd if they align with your usual diet. These suggestions serve as meal ideas rather than strict rules. Regional favorites can be enjoyed alongside sweets without making the day feel overly restrictive.

Keep drinks straightforward

Provide drinking water for guests as they cook and decorate. A well-known tea can be pleasant, but labeling a beverage as detoxifying does not prove it eliminates toxins or improves the skin. Costly powders and extreme cleansing routines are unnecessary extras for the celebration.

Make sweets part of hospitality

Serve a few pieces thoughtfully and allow guests to choose their portion. Offer an unsweetened drink and a savoury option as well. Avoid commenting on someone else's body or treating a festive dessert as a moral failure.

Plan for the busy hours

Prepare a simple meal ahead of evening worship. Those with medical conditions should maintain their regular food and medication routines instead of switching to an online festive diet. Prioritizing care is more beneficial than seeking immediate glow promises.

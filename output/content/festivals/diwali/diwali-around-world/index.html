About the series and its images

Diwali, Deepavali and Dipavali are used by different communities, and dates and meanings vary. Hindu, Sikh, Jain and other relevant observances should not be treated as one undifferentiated ritual. Several pieces also distinguish Diwali from neighboring light festivals such as Loi Krathong, Galungan and Thadingyut.

Illustrations come from the supplied festivals-diwali.imagelist, using rangoli, diyas, prayers, lighting and sweets. The images communicate Diwali themes; they are not documentary images of Nepal, London, Trinidad or the other places named.

Annual events, local regulations and current travel advisories need to be checked before making travel arrangements. Editorial notes and source-image lineage are included in the ZIP package.

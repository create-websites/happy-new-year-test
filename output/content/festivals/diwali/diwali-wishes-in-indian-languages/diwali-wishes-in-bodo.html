6 wishes

Review note: This is a short draft set in Devanagari Bodo : please have a native speaker review and expand it before posting. English meanings are under each wish.

  1. दिवालीनि खुलुमबाय! नोंथांनि न'आव सोरां, गोसोआव रंजानाय आरो जिउआव गोजोन थाथों। Diwali greetings! May there be light in your home, joy in your heart and peace in your life.

  2. बोसोर गासैआव नोंथांनि न'नि सुबुंफोरनि गेजेराव अननाय आरो सोरां बांसिन जाथों। May love and light grow among your family members all year round.

  3. लक्ष्मी आइनि आसिर्बाद नोंथांनि न'आव थाथों। May Mother Lakshmi's blessing stay in your home.

  4. आंनि लोगोफोरनो दिवालीनि रंजानाय खुलुमबाय : जिउनि गासै खोमसि खारथों। Joyful Diwali greetings to my friends : may all darkness flee from life.

  5. आबादारिफोरनो दिवालीनि जोबोद खुलुमबाय। Many Diwali greetings to the farmers.

  6. गोजोन आरो सोरांनि सान : दिवाली! A day of peace and light : Diwali!

13 wishes

Copy and share a festival greeting

  1. (Poetic) May the first diya you light tonight find its way into the quietest corner of your heart, may every sparkler write a wish across your sky, and long after the last flame flickers out, may the warmth of this Diwali stay curled up in your home like a cat by the hearth.

  2. (Storytelling) Centuries ago, an entire city lit lamps to welcome a prince home from exile, turning the darkest night of the month into its brightest; this Diwali, may every long wait in your life end in a homecoming just as glowing.

  3. (Letter) Dear friend, I couldn't send you a box of sweets across the miles this year, so here's a message carrying one small flame, a pinch of cardamom, the sound of temple bells and all my affection : set it on your windowsill and know we're under the same glow.

  4. (Nostalgic) Remember counting rockets from the rooftop, sneaking an extra laddoo before the puja and getting scolded for stepping on Mom's rangoli? May this Diwali bring back that same wide-eyed joy, only now with fewer burnt fingers and more grateful hearts.

  5. (Spiritual) May Goddess Lakshmi walk through your open door, Lord Ganesha clear every obstacle on your path, and the light of Saraswati keep your mind bright : so that prosperity, wisdom and peace sit together at your table all year long.

  6. (Motivational) A single diya never argues with the darkness; it simply keeps burning. This year, may you meet every challenge the same way : steady, warm and unafraid : because light is measured not by size but by persistence. Happy Diwali!

  7. (Gratitude) This Diwali, a heartfelt thank-you to the potters who shape our diyas, the shopkeepers who stay open late, the helpers who clean our homes and the cooks who fill our plates : your hands make our festival possible, and we wish your homes the brightest light of all.

  8. (Eco-friendly) Let's light clay lamps from a local potter, keep the crackers quiet and the laughter loud, share sweets with the neighbours we rarely greet, and give our elders, children, pets and the air we breathe a Diwali they'll truly enjoy.

  9. (Light-hearted) May your wallet be heavy with Lakshmi's blessings, your plate heavy with kaju katli, your phone heavy with forwarded wishes and your home heavy with guests : the only thing I hope stays light this Diwali is your bathroom scale.

  10. (For those far away) To everyone celebrating far from home, in cities where Diwali is just another workday: light one lamp by your window, call your mother before the aarti, and remember that tiny flame carries every prayer your family is saying for you tonight.

  11. (For those on duty) To the soldiers on the border, the nurses on night shift, the firefighters on alert and every worker who keeps the city running while we celebrate : we light our lamps safely because you stay awake; may your own Diwali be just as safe and bright.

  12. (Business) May the new ledger of the year open with auspicious numbers, every deal stand on a foundation of trust, and our partnership burn as steadily as a diya and its wick : warm Diwali wishes to you, your family and your entire team.

  13. (Inclusive) To our Sikh friends celebrating Bandi Chhor Divas, our Jain friends marking Mahavir Nirvan, our Bengali friends at Kali Puja and our Nepali friends in the middle of Tihar : the same night shines for all of us; may freedom, peace and light be yours.

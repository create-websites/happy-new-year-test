Bodhon and Chokhu Dan: Awakening the Goddess

Learn about Bodhon, the ceremonial awakening of Goddess Durga, and Chokhu Dan, the sacred artistic moment when the idol's eyes are finished.

Durga Puja Hub
Why this guide matters

Durga Puja is a celebration of transformation: clay transforms into image, image into presence, and artistic work into sacred focus. At the core of this metamorphosis are Bodhon and Chokhu Dan.

Bodhon: the ritual of awakening

Bodhon is the ritualistic awakening or calling upon Goddess Durga, marking the transition from preparation to the beginning of worship.

The ritual serves as a reminder to devotees that a festival is more than just decorations and gatherings; it is a structured event where time, location, and purpose hold significance.

Chokhu Dan: when the face comes alive

The focus of Chokhu Dan is squarely on the eyes. The artist's last touch on the Goddess's gaze is held in high esteem, as it alters the way observers perceive the idol.

Creatively, a few precise strokes can transform the mood, vitality, and impact of a piece. Spiritually, it appears as though the artwork is gazing upon the congregation assembled in front of it.

Art and ritual meet in one act

Durga Puja intricately intertwines craft with worship, as sculptors, painters, decorators, and priests all play a role in merging material expertise with spiritual significance.

This is why the idol is more than just a completed piece. Its creation is an integral part of the festival narrative.

Respectful viewing for visitors

Guests are encouraged to observe and appreciate these rituals without crowding the artists or disrupting worship. Photography must adhere to the guidelines set by the workshop, household, or pandal.

Viewing Bodhon and Chokhu Dan not as mere spectacles to be collected, but as portals revealing the interconnectedness of community, art, and devotion, is the most fulfilling approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What does Bodhon mean?

This line describes the ceremonial awakening or invocation of Goddess Durga for worship.

What is Chokhu Dan?

It represents the divine essence of the Goddess, a crucial step in the creation of idols.

Are Bodhon and Chokhu Dan the same ritual?

No, these are separate instances, each highlighting the shift from readiness to sacred presence.

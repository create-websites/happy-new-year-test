Why Durga Puja matters

A festival where worship becomes a citywide work of art

During Durga Puja, sacred rituals, temporary structures, idol making, music, food, fashion, and community involvement converge. In Bengal, the Goddess is revered as a powerful cosmic entity and a beloved daughter coming back home.

This hub categorizes 20 guides into four groups, allowing readers to navigate from understanding rituals to exploring craftsmanship, community involvement, and hands-on Pujo experiences.

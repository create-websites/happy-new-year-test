Why Durga Puja matters

A festival where worship becomes a citywide work of art

Durga Puja brings together sacred ritual, temporary architecture, idol making, music, food, fashion and neighborhood participation. In Bengal, the Goddess is celebrated as both a cosmic force and a daughter returning home.

This hub organizes 20 guides into four clusters so readers can move from ritual meaning to craft, community life and practical Pujo experiences.

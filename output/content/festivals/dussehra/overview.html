Why Vijayadashami matters

One festival, many forms of victory

Dussehra symbolizes Rama's triumph over Ravana, Durga's defeat of Mahishasura, the revival of education during Vidyarambham, the reverence for tools in Shastra or Ayudha Puja, or a cultural festival honoring local deities and traditions.

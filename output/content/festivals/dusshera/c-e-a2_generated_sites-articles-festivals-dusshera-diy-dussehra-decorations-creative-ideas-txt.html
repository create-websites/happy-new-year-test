Why Vijayadashami matters

One festival, many forms of victory

Dussehra can mean Rama’s victory over Ravana, Durga’s victory over Mahishasura, the return to learning through Vidyarambham, the honoring of tools through Shastra or Ayudha Puja, or a regional celebration centered on local deities and heritage.

This hub organizes 20 guides into four clusters so readers can move from myth and ritual to travel, reflection and practical celebration.

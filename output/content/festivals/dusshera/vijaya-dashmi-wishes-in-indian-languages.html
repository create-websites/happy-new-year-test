About the festival

Vijaya Dashami and Dussehra Wishes

Vijaya Dashami, widely celebrated as Dussehra, is expressed in these greetings through themes of dharma over adharma, truth over falsehood, the blessings of Lord Rama, the symbolic burning of Ravana, and wishes for happiness, peace, prosperity and success.

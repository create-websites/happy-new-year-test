Frequently asked questions

How many festivals and languages are covered?

Twelve festivals. New Year, Valentine's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas each have 100 world languages. The eight Indian festivals have 38 languages each: 28 Indian languages (all 22 on India's Eighth Schedule plus six widely spoken ones) and 10 world languages, with Diwali adding 10 more world and diaspora languages.

Can I copy a greeting?

Yes. Open a festival, search or filter by region, and press Copy on a card to copy the greeting in its own script.

When are these festivals in 2027?

Calendar order: New Year on January 1, Lohri on January 13, Makar Sankranti and Pongal around January 14 to 15, Valentine's Day on February 14, Maha Shivratri on March 6, Rakhi on August 17, Onam on September 12, Dussehra on October 9, Diwali on October 29, Thanksgiving on November 25 and Christmas on December 25. Festivals that follow the moon can differ by a day by region.

Are the translations checked by native speakers?

The greetings are standard or published forms where available, but the pages have not been reviewed by a speaker of every language, and cards that are less certain are marked on the card.

Festival context

About Dussehra and Vijayadashami

Dussehra, also called Vijayadashami, celebrates the triumph of good over evil in traditions across India and neighboring communities. These multilingual cards make it easier to send a familiar greeting to family, friends and colleagues in the language they use most.

The translations and festival card artwork from the supplied collection are preserved; this version focuses on a clearer rose-and-glass Happy New Year visual system, simpler navigation and stronger mobile usability.

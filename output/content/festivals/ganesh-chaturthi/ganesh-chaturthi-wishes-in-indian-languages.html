About the festival

Ganesh Chaturthi Wishes and Blessings

These Ganesh Chaturthi greetings celebrate Lord Ganesha as the bringer of happiness, peace and prosperity and as Vighnaharta, the remover of obstacles. The collection also includes affectionate Bappa messages and festive wishes inspired by modak and Ganeshotsav.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Indian languages are included?

Hindi, Bhojpuri, Rajasthani, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Odia, Kannada, Malayalam, Urdu, Assamese, Pali, Sanskrit, Haryanvi, Awadhi and Garhwali.

What kinds of Ganesh Chaturthi greetings are included?

The collection includes Lord Ganesha and Bappa blessings, Vighnaharta wishes for removing obstacles, prosperity and family greetings, and festive modak and Ganeshotsav messages.

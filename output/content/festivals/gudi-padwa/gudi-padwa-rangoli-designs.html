10 Rangoli Ideas for Gudi Padwa

Welcome the Maharashtrian New Year with simple rangoli concepts inspired by the Gudi, lotus, mango leaves, sunbursts, kalash forms and geometric borders.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Clothing and home décor turn Gudi Padwa into a visible celebration. These guides focus on approachable styling and decorations that feel festive without becoming difficult to execute.

Why rangoli belongs at the entrance

Rangoli marks the threshold as a place of welcome. On Gudi Padwa, it visually connects the cleaned doorway, toran, raised Gudi and arriving guests. Even a small design can make the entrance feel ceremonial.

Choose a scale that fits your doorway and the amount of foot traffic. A compact design that remains intact is better than a large one everyone must step over.

1–3: Gudi, lotus and kalash motifs

  1. Mini Gudi: draw a vertical banner shape with leaves and a small vessel at the top.
  2. Eight-petal lotus: use a simple central flower with alternating saffron and white petals.
  3. Kalash geometry: combine a pot outline with mango-leaf shapes and a radiating border.

4–6: Sunburst, mango leaves and peacock accents

  1. Sunburst: create concentric circles with rays to suggest a bright beginning.
  2. Mango-leaf border: repeat green leaf shapes around a square or semicircle.
  3. Peacock corner motif: use one stylized peacock at the edge rather than a full complex bird.

7–10: Dots, florals and modern minimal patterns

  1. Dot-grid rangoli: build a symmetrical pattern from a small grid.
  2. Marigold petal rangoli: use real flower petals for a low-waste design.
  3. White line rangoli: keep the palette minimal and let geometry do the work.
  4. Family initials + Gudi border: personalize a modern design while keeping a festival symbol.

Color palette ideas

Saffron, yellow, green, white and deep red work beautifully for Gudi Padwa. If your Gudi cloth has a dominant color, repeat that tone in the rangoli to tie the whole entrance together.

Natural flower petals and reusable rangoli stencils are good options for families who want less synthetic color powder.

Make it child-friendly

Give children one defined section rather than the whole design. They can fill petals, place flower heads, or use a stencil. This produces a cleaner final result while still giving them ownership.

Always keep powders away from very young children and pets, especially if ingredients are not clearly labeled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Which colors suit a Gudi Padwa rangoli?

Saffron, yellow, green, white and red pair naturally with the festival’s decorations.

What is the easiest rangoli for beginners?

A small lotus, sunburst or dot-grid design is usually easier than a large freehand scene.

Can flowers replace rangoli powder?

Yes. Marigold and other petals can create a bright, low-waste entrance design.

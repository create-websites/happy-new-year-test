Why Gudi Padwa matters

A New Year raised like a banner

Observed on Chaitra Shukla Pratipada, Gudi Padwa marks the Maharashtrian New Year. The festival brings together calendrical renewal, mythology, family ritual, seasonal food, public celebration and a powerful visual symbol—the Gudi raised high outside the home.

This hub organizes 20 practical and cultural guides into five clusters so you can move from meaning to celebration: learn the stories first, understand the rituals, plan the meal, dress and decorate the home, then explore sustainable and diaspora adaptations.

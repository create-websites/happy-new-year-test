Men’s Gudi Padwa Fashion: Kurta and Pheta

Achieve a sophisticated Maharashtrian festive ensemble by pairing a kurta or dhoti with a Nehru jacket and a traditional pheta, all while prioritizing comfort.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Gudi Padwa becomes a vibrant celebration with clothing and home décor taking center stage. These guides emphasize easy-to-achieve styling and decorations that exude festivity without being overly complicated.

Start with the occasion

Before choosing your outfit, consider the duration and activity level of events like a home puja, neighborhood procession, cultural program, and formal community event, as they may require varying levels of dress.

A well-fitting kurta made of breathable fabric can look more sophisticated than a heavily embroidered piece that quickly becomes uncomfortable.

Kurta colors for the festival

The colors of saffron, cream, white, green, maroon, and gold tones complement Gudi Padwa's aesthetic. Solid colors look great when photographed alongside rangoli, marigolds, and a vibrant Gudi.

To ensure the pheta stands out, opt for a more understated kurta to avoid conflicting styles in the outfit.

Pajama, churidar or dhoti

A straight pajama or churidar is a simple choice for many. Opting for a dhoti-style bottom can add a more traditional touch, but it is important to securely tie it and practice beforehand, especially for longer events.

Pre-sewn dhoti pants are a convenient option for younger participants and diaspora gatherings.

The pheta as the focal point

The pheta, a traditional Maharashtrian turban, has the power to elevate a basic kurta into a unique festive ensemble. Saffron is a favored choice for public events and parades, but various colors and regional tying techniques are also embraced.

If you're unsure how to tie a tie, seek help from a knowledgeable family member or opt for a pre-tied option to ensure a reliable, proper fit.

Add a jacket only when needed

A Nehru jacket adds shape to a simple kurta and is perfect for formal occasions or photoshoots. But in warm weather, an extra layer may not be needed.

Good festive dressing includes comfortable clothing and suitable footwear for walking. Opt for breathable fabrics.

Coordinate without becoming identical

Many families choose to match colors for photos, but it's more stylish to opt for a coordinated palette like saffron and cream, green and gold, or maroon and beige.

This allows for each person to wear a style that suits them while still appearing cohesive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is a pheta?

A pheta is a Maharashtrian turban worn for special events, celebrations, and cultural gatherings.

Does a Gudi Padwa outfit require a dhoti?

Wearing a kurta with pajama, churidar, or trousers can create a festive look, while a dhoti is a traditional choice.

Can I use a pre-tied pheta?

Yes, it can be useful if you lack someone skilled in tying the traditional method.

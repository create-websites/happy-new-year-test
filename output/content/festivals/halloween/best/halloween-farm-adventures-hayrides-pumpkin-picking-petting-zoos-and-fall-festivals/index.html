Halloween Farm Adventures: Hayrides, Pumpkin Picking, Petting Zoos and Fall Festivals

America's farms have turned autumn into a festival season. Hayrides, apple cider, baby goats and pumpkin fields: here's what to expect at a fall farm and how to get the most from it.

Key takeaways

  • Classic farm fun: hayrides, pick-your-own pumpkins and apples, corn mazes and petting zoos.
  • Cider mills and cider donuts are a fall rite of passage.
  • Evenings bring haunted hayrides and bonfires : check age guidance.
  • Wash hands after animals, stay on paths and carry pumpkins from the bottom.

Autumn on an American farm feels like stepping into a storybook : red barns, golden fields, the smell of cider and woodsmoke, and the bump of a hay wagon on a dirt track. In recent decades thousands of farms have opened their gates in fall, creating a whole season of agritourism around Halloween. Here's what you'll find and how to enjoy it.

Hayrides

The hayride is the quintessential fall farm experience. Visitors sit on hay bales in a wagon pulled by a tractor (or occasionally horses) for a tour of the farm or a trip out to the pumpkin field.

  • Daytime hayrides : relaxing and family-friendly; some include commentary about the farm.
  • Sunset rides : beautiful light for photos.
  • Haunted hayrides : after dark, with actors and effects along the trail; best for teens and adults.

Tip: Sit near the front for a smoother ride, or the back for the best views.

Pumpkin picking

Picking your own pumpkin off the vine is the main event at most fall farms. Bring garden clippers if allowed, or use the farm's. Look for a firm green stem and no soft spots, and never carry it by the stem. Learn more about varieties in From Pumpkin Patch to Jack-o'-Lantern, and find farms in America's Best Pumpkin Patches.

Apple orchards

Many farms combine pumpkins with pick-your-own apples. Orchards post what's ripe each week : early varieties in late summer, classics like McIntosh, Honeycrisp and Fuji in September and October.

Picking technique: lift the apple and give a gentle upward twist rather than pulling : it protects next year's buds.

Cider mills and donuts

Fresh-pressed apple cider and hot cider donuts, often rolled in cinnamon sugar, are a fall pilgrimage for many families. Some mills let visitors watch the press. Pair cider with kettle corn, caramel apples or a slice of pie.

Petting zoos

Goats, sheep, alpacas, rabbits, chickens and pigs are favorites with small children. Some farms offer goat walks, bottle-feeding or pony rides.

Hygiene matters: Animals can carry germs such as E. coli. Wash hands with soap and water (not just sanitizer) after touching animals, don't eat in animal areas, and keep pacifiers and sippy cups away.

Corn mazes and hay mazes

Big farms cut elaborate corn mazes; smaller ones build hay-bale mazes for little kids. See The Ultimate Corn Maze Guide.

Evening farm events

As Halloween approaches, many farms add after-dark experiences:

  • Haunted hayrides and corn mazes for thrill-seekers (America's Best Haunted Houses covers the scariest attractions)
  • Flashlight mazes for families
  • Bonfires and s'mores
  • Jack-o'-lantern trails lit with carved pumpkins
  • Movie nights in the field

Farm etiquette

  • Stay on paths; fields and orchards are working crops.
  • Pay for what you pick : sampling is often discouraged.
  • Leave pets at home unless the farm allows them.
  • Supervise children around animals and machinery.
  • Follow staff instructions on hayrides : remain seated and keep hands inside.
  • Be patient : farms are busy in October.

Questions & answers

What do you do at a fall festival on a farm?

Typical activities include hayrides to the pumpkin patch, picking pumpkins and apples, corn and hay-bale mazes, petting zoos, giant slides and jumping pillows, pig races, apple cannons, live music, and food such as cider donuts, kettle corn and caramel apples.

What is a haunted hayride?

A haunted hayride is an evening wagon ride through woods or fields where costumed actors, special effects and staged scenes create scares along the route. They are usually intended for teens and adults.

Are petting zoos safe for kids?

Petting zoos are generally safe with good hygiene. Animals can carry germs such as E. coli, so wash hands thoroughly with soap and water after touching animals, don't eat or drink in animal areas, and supervise young children who put hands in their mouths.

When is apple-picking season?

In most of the northern U.S., apple-picking runs from late August through October, with many popular varieties ripening in September. Check your local orchard for what is ripe each week.

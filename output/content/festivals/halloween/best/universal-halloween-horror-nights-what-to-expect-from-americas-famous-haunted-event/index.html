Universal Halloween Horror Nights: What to Expect from America's Famous Haunted Event

Elaborate haunted houses built with film-studio budgets, scare zones packed with actors and fog, and crowds who come specifically to be terrified. Here's what to expect at HHN.

Key takeaways

  • HHN 35 runs select nights Aug 28–Nov 1, 2026 in Orlando and Sept 3–Nov 1 in Hollywood.
  • Expect film-quality haunted houses, scare zones, live shows and themed food.
  • Universal says the event may not be suitable for children under 13.
  • Go on a weeknight early in the season, arrive at opening and consider an Express pass.

For horror fans, there's Halloween : and then there's Halloween Horror Nights. Universal's flagship fall event transforms its theme parks into a nighttime labyrinth of haunted houses, fog-filled streets and costumed scare actors. In 2026 the event celebrates its 35th edition.

Dates for 2026

Location Dates
Universal Orlando Resort (Universal Studios Florida) Select nights, August 28 – November 1, 2026
Universal Studios Hollywood Select nights, September 3 – November 1, 2026

The Orlando run starts earlier than ever, reflecting how popular the event has become. Halloween itself falls on a Saturday this year, so expect peak crowds that weekend.

A short history

HHN began in 1991 at Universal Studios Florida as a smaller event called Fright Nights, adopting the Halloween Horror Nights name the following year. Over three decades it has grown into one of the largest Halloween events in the world, known for:

  • Original "icon" characters created by Universal's creative team
  • Haunted houses based on horror films and TV, built with movie-studio set design
  • Scare zones where streets are themed and filled with actors

What to expect

Haunted houses

The main attraction. Each house is an indoor walk-through built around a story : sometimes based on famous horror franchises, sometimes entirely original. Expect detailed sets, practical effects, sound design, smells, animatronics and actors timed to hit you from every angle. Orlando typically features around ten houses, with a slightly smaller lineup in Hollywood.

Scare zones

Outdoor areas themed to a story, with roaming actors who target guests walking through. You can't skip them if you want to cross the park, which is part of the fun.

Shows and entertainment

Live shows, from comedic revues to stunt and dance performances, offer a break from the screaming.

Hollywood's backlot

Universal Studios Hollywood's version has traditionally included scares on its famous studio backlot, such as the Terror Tram.

Food and drink

Themed snacks, specialty cocktails and limited-time treats are part of the experience : and a good way to rest between houses.

Who it's for

Universal advises that HHN may not be suitable for children under 13. The houses include gore, intense violence, darkness, strobes and loud noises. Pregnant guests and those with heart conditions or sensitivities to fog and strobes should also take care.

For a family-friendly alternative, see Disney Halloween Celebrations.

Tickets and passes

  • Single-night tickets : prices vary by date; weeknights and early dates are cheaper.
  • Frequent-visit passes : for multiple nights.
  • Express passes : skip the regular lines, once per house; sell out on peak nights.
  • Guided tours : premium tours with escorted access, reserved seating and extras.
  • Hotel and early-entry options in Orlando can let you start before the general crowd.

How to plan your night

  1. Pick a weeknight early in the season. Lines are shortest on Sunday–Thursday in September.
  2. Arrive before gates open. The first hour has the shortest waits.
  3. Use the app to check live wait times.
  4. Prioritize the most popular houses first, or save them for the last hour when lines often drop.
  5. Eat beforehand or between houses : food lines grow late in the evening.
  6. Wear comfortable shoes : you'll walk miles.
  7. Travel light : check bag policies; lockers may be required.
  8. Pace yourself : houses are intense; build in shows and snacks.

Rules to know

  • No guest costumes or masks : so actors and guests are easy to tell apart.
  • Don't touch the actors, and they won't touch you.
  • Follow bag and security rules.
  • Strobe and fog are used heavily.

How does HHN compare?

Halloween Horror Nights is often compared with Knott's Scary Farm in Southern California, Busch Gardens' Howl-O-Scream, and Six Flags Fright Fest. See Halloween at Amusement Parks for a side-by-side comparison.

Make it a trip

In Orlando, combine HHN with daytime park visits and a side trip to historic St. Augustine (St. Augustine, Florida). In California, pair Hollywood with Knott's and Disneyland on a California Halloween Road Trip. And if you want to keep the scares going across several stops, see Planning a Haunted Attraction Road Trip.

Questions & answers

When is Halloween Horror Nights 2026?

Halloween Horror Nights 2026, the event's 35th edition, runs on select nights from August 28 through November 1 at Universal Orlando Resort and from September 3 through November 1 at Universal Studios Hollywood.

Is Halloween Horror Nights good for kids?

Universal advises that Halloween Horror Nights may not be appropriate for children under 13. It is designed for teens and adults, with intense scares, gore and strobe effects.

How many haunted houses are at Halloween Horror Nights?

Universal Orlando typically features around ten haunted houses plus several scare zones, while Hollywood's lineup is usually somewhat smaller. Check Universal's announcements for the current year's exact lineup.

Can you wear a costume to Halloween Horror Nights?

Guests are generally not permitted to wear costumes or masks at Halloween Horror Nights, so scare actors can be distinguished from visitors. Check Universal's current guest policies before you go.

Universal reveals house lineups, hours and prices on its own schedule. Confirm current details, age guidance and park policies on Universal's official websites.

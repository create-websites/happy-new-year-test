Decoration inspiration

Make an entrance

Start with the entrance that guests will see first. Combine a simple focal point with enough light to keep the route clear and welcoming.

Choose a few consistent shapes and textures rather than filling every space. Repeated elements can tie a doorway, table and porch together.

Hands-on activities

Create the little details

Small craft projects are an easy way to include children and friends in the preparations. Paper decorations, handmade cards and pumpkin-themed designs can be personalized without complicated materials.

Make an activity area with simple supplies, clear instructions and a spot where finished creations can be displayed.

Hosting tips for a welcoming Halloween

Choose decorations suitable for your setting and secure anything that could fall or block a path. Battery-powered lights can offer atmosphere without placing an open flame next to paper or fabric. For costumes, visibility and easy movement are more important than an elaborate finish.

For treats and shared food, make ingredient information easy to find and include a non-food option when practical. For young children, plan a clear end time and a calmer area if the celebration becomes noisy.

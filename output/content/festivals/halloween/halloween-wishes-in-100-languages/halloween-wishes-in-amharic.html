Wishing you a night full of fun and laughter.

Wish 7

Have a sweet Halloween full of candy.
Wish 9

Wishing you a magical Halloween night.

Don't let the ghosts scare you tonight!

May the only scares tonight be fun ones.

Trick or treat! Have a wonderful Halloween.
Wish 13

Wishing you the best costume of the night.
Wish 14

May your night be full of spooky surprises.
Wish 15

Have a hauntingly happy Halloween!
Wish 16

Wishing the little ones a fun and safe trick-or-treat.

ሃሎዊንዎ ከአስፈሪነቱ ይልቅ ጣፋጭነቱ ይብዛ።
May your Halloween be more sweet than scary.
Wish 18
ዛሬ ማታ በእያንዳንዱ ከረሜላና በእያንዳንዱ ሳቅ ይደሰቱ።
Enjoy every treat and every laugh tonight.
Wish 19

Watch out for witches and black cats tonight!
Wish 20
ከጓደኞችዎ ጋር ማራኪ ምሽት እመኝልዎታለሁ።
Wishing you a bewitching evening with friends.
Wish 21
ሙሉ ጨረቃ መልካም ዕድል ያምጣልዎ።
May the full moon bring you good luck.
Wish 22
ዛሬ ማታ የሚጎበኙዎት ወዳጃዊ መናፍስት ብቻ ይሁኑ።
May only friendly ghosts visit you tonight.
Wish 23
እያንዳንዱን ሃሎዊን የበለጠ አስደሳች ስላደረጉ አመሰግናለሁ። መልካም ሃሎዊን!
Thank you for making every Halloween more fun. Happy Halloween!
Wish 24
በዚህ ሃሎዊን ደህና ይሁኑ፣ ይደሰቱም።
Stay safe and have fun this Halloween.
Wish 25
ከመናፍስት ጋር መልካም ጊዜ ይሁንልዎ!
Have a ghostly good time!
Wish 26
ዛሬ ማታ ብዙ ከረሜላ ይብሉ፣ ብዙም ይሳቁ።
Eat lots of candy and laugh a lot tonight.
Wish 27
ለደስተኛ ሃሎዊን መልካም ምኞቴን እገልጻለሁ!

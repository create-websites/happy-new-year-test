Best Halloween Costume Ideas

Witches, vampires and superheroes still rule : but the most memorable costumes are the ones with a clever idea behind them. Ideas for every age, budget and group size.

At a glance

According to the National Retail Federation's 2026 Halloween survey, the top children's costumes are Spider-Man, superhero, princess, witch and ghost; the top adult costumes are witch, vampire, Batman, Spider-Man and ghost; and the top pet costumes are pumpkin, hot dog and ghost. Strong costume ideas fall into categories such as classic monsters, occupations, food, puns, nature and coordinated themes for couples, families and groups. Choose costumes that are comfortable, weather-appropriate, visible at night and easy to move in.

Choosing a costume is half the fun of Halloween. Some people plan for months; others raid the closet at 5 p.m. on October 31. Either way, the best costumes share a few qualities: they're instantly readable, comfortable to wear and a little bit clever.

Key takeaways

  • 2026's top costumes (NRF): kids : Spider-Man, superhero, princess, witch, ghost; adults : witch, vampire, Batman, Spider-Man, ghost.
  • Pets: pumpkin is the top pet costume, followed by hot dog and ghost.
  • The best group costumes share one strong theme, so each person's piece makes sense together.
  • Plan for weather, visibility and movement : especially for kids walking in the dark.

Costume ideas for kids

Timeless favorites

  • Witch or wizard with a hat, cape and wand
  • Friendly ghost (add a pumpkin bucket for color)
  • Superhero with a homemade emblem
  • Princess, knight or dragon
  • Pirate with an eye patch and treasure map
  • Astronaut in a white jumpsuit with NASA-style patches

Animals

  • Black cat, owl, bat or spider
  • Dinosaur with a hooded tail
  • Lion, tiger or bear
  • Bumblebee or ladybug for the youngest

Imagination

  • Construction worker, firefighter, doctor or chef
  • Crayon box (each child a different color)
  • Robot from boxes and silver tape
  • Mad scientist with goggles and a lab coat

Tip for parents: Have kids try their costume on a week early. Check that they can walk, climb stairs, see clearly and carry a bucket. More in Halloween Costume Safety.

Costume ideas for adults

Classic monsters

Witches and vampires top the list for a reason : they're glamorous and easy. Other evergreen options: werewolf, mummy, zombie, skeleton, Frankenstein's monster, grim reaper.

Clever and pun-based

  • "Smart cookie" : chef's hat and cookies with glasses
  • "Social butterfly" : butterfly wings covered in app logos drawn by hand
  • "Ceiling fan" : gray shirt and pom-poms cheering for the ceiling
  • "Holy cow" : cow ears with a halo
  • "Deviled egg" : egg costume with devil horns

Historical and literary

  • A Victorian ghost, a 1920s flapper, a medieval plague doctor
  • Characters from classic literature: Dracula, the Headless Horseman, Sherlock Holmes

Low-effort, high-impact

  • All black plus cat ears and eyeliner whiskers
  • Sheet ghost with sunglasses
  • Vampire cape and fake teeth over formal wear

Couples costumes

The best couples costumes are pairs that need each other to make sense:

  • Salt and pepper
  • Peanut butter and jelly
  • Vampire and victim
  • Witch and black cat
  • Scarecrow and crow
  • Gardener and pumpkin
  • Lighthouse keeper and sea monster
  • Two halves of a heart, a puzzle or a pumpkin
  • Classic movie duos and famous historical pairs

Family costumes

A family theme gives everyone a role and makes great photos:

  • The farm: farmer, scarecrow, pumpkin, cow and chicken
  • The haunted house: ghosts of different sizes, with the baby as the smallest
  • The solar system: sun, planets and a toddler as the moon
  • A deck of cards: king, queen, jack and ace
  • The circus: ringmaster, lion, acrobat, clown
  • The garden: gardener, flowers, bee and a ladybug
  • A favorite storybook: each person a character

Planning a trunk-or-treat? Match your family costumes to your car's theme using the ideas in Trunk-or-Treat Explained.

Group costumes

For friend groups, offices and parties:

  • Crayons or highlighters : each a different color
  • A box of chocolates : each person a different flavor
  • The seasons or the weather
  • Classic monsters squad : vampire, werewolf, mummy, witch, ghost
  • A full board game : pieces from chess or a favorite family game
  • Emojis : printed faces on plain T-shirts

The trick is one clear theme and consistent quality : if half the group commits and half doesn't, the theme gets lost.

Pet costumes

Pumpkins (11.6% of pet owners), hot dogs (5.9%) and ghosts (2.8%) led the NRF's 2026 pet list. A few rules for happy pets:

  • Choose costumes that don't restrict movement, breathing, vision or hearing.
  • Avoid small pieces that can be chewed and swallowed.
  • Try it on briefly first : some pets simply hate costumes, and a festive bandana is fine.
  • Keep pets away from the front door and candy on Halloween night.

How to choose your costume

Ask yourself:

  1. Where am I going? A party, a parade, trick-or-treating or a workplace each call for different levels of comfort and decorum.
  2. What's the weather? Late October can be cold and wet; plan layers.
  3. How long will I wear it? Masks and big headpieces get tiring fast.
  4. Can people tell what I am? The best costumes read in under three seconds.
  5. Is it respectful? Avoid costumes that mock real cultures, religions or tragedies.

Make it memorable

You don't need the most expensive costume to win the night. A strong idea, a few well-chosen props and confident commitment will always beat a bag-in-a-box costume. Then take it to a party : How to Host the Ultimate Halloween Party has tips for running a costume contest.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most popular Halloween costumes in 2026?

The NRF's 2026 survey lists Spider-Man, superhero, princess, witch and ghost as the top children's costumes, and witch, vampire, Batman, Spider-Man and ghost as the top adult costumes. Pumpkin, hot dog and ghost lead for pets.

What is a good last-minute costume?

A ghost sheet, a witch with a hat and dark clothes, a vampire with a cape and slicked hair, a black cat with ears and eyeliner whiskers, or a pun costume made from household items can all be assembled in under an hour.

How do I pick a family Halloween costume?

Choose a theme with enough roles for everyone, such as a movie cast, a farm, a deck of cards, the solar system or a food set. Let kids pick their character within the theme and keep the baby or pet as the centerpiece.

How much do Americans spend on costumes?

The NRF projected about $1.5 billion on children's costumes, $2 billion on adult costumes and about $0.92 billion on pet costumes in 2026.

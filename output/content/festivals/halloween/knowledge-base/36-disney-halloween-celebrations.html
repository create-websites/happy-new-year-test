Disney Halloween Celebrations

Disney does Halloween the Disney way: more charm than fright, with candy trails, villains, parades and a castle that glows orange. Here's what to expect in 2026 on both coasts.

At a glance

Disney's U.S. parks celebrate Halloween with family-friendly, separately ticketed parties. At Walt Disney World, Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party runs select nights from August 7 to October 31, 2026, at Magic Kingdom, featuring Mickey's Boo-to-You Halloween Parade, the Disney's Not So Spooky Spectacular fireworks hosted by Jack Skellington, trick-or-treat stations and a new 'A Masquerade with Stitch' dance party. At Disneyland Resort, the Oogie Boogie Bash at Disney California Adventure runs select nights from August 18 to October 31, 2026, from 6 to 11 p.m., with Madame Leota's Swinging Wake street party and the Villains Grove walk-through. Guests of all ages may wear costumes at the parties, within Disney's costume rules.

If you love Halloween but don't love being chased by chainsaw-wielding actors, Disney may be your perfect October destination. Both of Disney's U.S. resorts host separately ticketed Halloween parties that trade terror for trick-or-treating, villains, parades and a lot of candy.

Key takeaways

  • Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party : Magic Kingdom, select nights Aug 7–Oct 31, 2026.
  • Oogie Boogie Bash : Disney California Adventure, select nights Aug 18–Oct 31, 2026, 6–11 p.m.
  • Both parties include trick-or-treat trails, Halloween entertainment and character meet-and-greets.
  • Adults can wear costumes at the parties : but read Disney's costume rules first.

Walt Disney World: Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party

When and where

Magic Kingdom, on select nights from August 7 to October 31, 2026. The party is a separately ticketed, after-hours event held once the park closes to regular day guests. Party guests can usually enter in the late afternoon.

What's on

  • Mickey's Boo-to-You Halloween Parade : the party's signature parade, led in tradition by the Headless Horseman galloping down Main Street, U.S.A. Reporting on the 2026 lineup lists it at 8:15 p.m.
  • Disney's Not So Spooky Spectacular : a fireworks show hosted by Jack Skellington, with projections on Cinderella Castle.
  • A Masquerade with Stitch : a new 2026 dance party at the Rockettower Plaza Stage in Tomorrowland, where Stitch, Lilo and Angel change the party's theme using items from Lilo's costume trunk.
  • DJ dance parties with surprise character appearances.
  • Trick-or-treat stations : more than a dozen locations on the party map in 2026.
  • Rare character meet-and-greets in Halloween costumes.
  • Party-exclusive food, including special menu items at Cinderella's Royal Table.
  • Halloween ride overlays and shorter waits on many attractions.

Tips

  • Arrive early : party guests can often enter in the afternoon.
  • Prioritize the parade, fireworks and rare characters; candy is everywhere.
  • Bring a bag for treats : you'll collect a lot.
  • Pick a quieter night : earlier dates and weeknights usually have lighter crowds.

Disneyland Resort: Halloween Time and Oogie Boogie Bash

Halloween Time

During Halloween Time, Disneyland Resort transforms with pumpkin-filled Main Street décor, seasonal food and the Haunted Mansion Holiday overlay, which fills the mansion with characters from The Nightmare Before Christmas. These experiences are included with regular park admission. Check Disney's site for the season's dates.

Oogie Boogie Bash

When: select nights from August 18 to October 31, 2026 Where: Disney California Adventure Hours: 6 to 11 p.m., with guests able to enter the park from 3 p.m. on party nights

Highlights for 2026 include:

  • Madame Leota's Swinging Wake – A Haunted Mansion Street Party : a lively street celebration themed to the Haunted Mansion.
  • Villains Grove : an atmospheric after-dark walk-through at the Redwood Creek Challenge Trail featuring Disney villains.
  • Treat trails around the park.
  • Villain and character meet-and-greets.

Oogie Boogie Bash is famously popular, and several 2026 dates sold out over the summer. Book as early as possible.

Costume rules

At both parties guests of all ages can wear costumes, but Disney has rules : for example, restrictions on masks, items resembling weapons, and costumes that drag on the ground or obstruct others. Outside the parties, guests 14 and older generally can't wear costumes in the parks. Check Disney's current costume guidelines before you pack.

Disney Halloween around the world

Disney's international parks also celebrate, with Halloween festivals at Disneyland Paris, Tokyo Disney Resort and Hong Kong Disneyland, each with its own parades, décor and characters.

Is Disney Halloween worth it?

Yes, if: you love Disney characters, want a not-so-scary Halloween for young kids, or want shorter ride lines and exclusive entertainment.

Maybe not, if: you're looking for genuine scares. In that case, look at Universal Halloween Horror Nights or America's Best Haunted Houses.

Frequently asked questions

When is Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party in 2026?

Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party runs on select nights from August 7 through October 31, 2026, at Magic Kingdom in Walt Disney World. It is a separately ticketed event held after the park closes to regular guests.

When is Oogie Boogie Bash 2026?

Oogie Boogie Bash takes place on select nights from August 18 through October 31, 2026, at Disney California Adventure, from 6 to 11 p.m., with entry to the park allowed from 3 p.m. on party nights. Several dates have sold out.

Can adults wear costumes at Disney Halloween parties?

Yes. Guests of all ages may wear costumes during Disney's Halloween parties, subject to rules on items such as masks, weapons-like props and costumes that drag on the ground. Outside the parties, guests 14 and older generally may not wear costumes in the parks.

Is Disney Halloween scary for kids?

No. Disney's parties are designed for families, with friendly characters, candy and lighthearted spooky themes. They are very different from scare-focused events like Universal's Halloween Horror Nights.

Halloween at Amusement Parks

Amusement parks host two very different kinds of Halloween: daytime candy trails for little ones and after-dark events built entirely on fear. Here's how to choose.

3 min readGuide 4 of 5 in this topic

Planning note. Event names, dates, age guidance and inclusions change every year. Check each park's official website for the current season.

At a glance

U.S. amusement parks offer two kinds of Halloween events. Family events : such as Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party at Magic Kingdom, Oogie Boogie Bash at Disney California Adventure, and daytime Halloween festivals at parks like SeaWorld, LEGOLAND and Six Flags : focus on trick-or-treating, characters and mild spooks. After-dark scare events : including Universal's Halloween Horror Nights, Knott's Scary Farm (since 1973), Busch Gardens' Howl-O-Scream and Six Flags' Fright Fest nights : feature haunted mazes, scare zones and actors, and are generally recommended for teens and adults. Many parks run both, with family activities by day and scares after dark.

Halloween is one of the biggest seasons of the year for American amusement parks. But "Halloween at the park" can mean two totally different things: a sunny afternoon collecting candy from costumed characters, or a fog-choked night dodging chainsaw-wielding actors. Choosing the right one makes all the difference.

Key takeaways

  • Family events: trick-or-treating, characters, mild décor : great for under-12s.
  • Scare nights: mazes, scare zones and actors : typically for teens and adults.
  • Many parks run both: family fun by day, scares after dark.
  • Go on weeknights early in the season and consider express passes for scare events.

Family-friendly Halloween events

Disney

  • Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party at Magic Kingdom (select nights, Aug 7–Oct 31, 2026) : parade, fireworks, treats and rare characters.
  • Oogie Boogie Bash at Disney California Adventure (select nights, Aug 18–Oct 31, 2026) : villains, treat trails, Haunted Mansion street party.
  • Halloween Time at Disneyland : décor and Haunted Mansion Holiday with regular admission.

Full details in Disney Halloween Celebrations.

Other family events

Many parks run daytime Halloween festivals with trick-or-treat trails, costume parades, pumpkin patches, hay mazes and Halloween shows:

  • SeaWorld parks : family Halloween festivals with trick-or-treating.
  • LEGOLAND parks : kid-focused Halloween events designed for younger children.
  • Six Flags and other regional parks : family-friendly daytime Halloween activities, with scares reserved for after dark.
  • Dollywood (Tennessee) : its fall festival and pumpkin-lantern nights blend harvest charm with gentle spooks.

After-dark scare events

Universal's Halloween Horror Nights

The 35th edition runs select nights Aug 28–Nov 1, 2026 in Orlando and Sept 3–Nov 1 in Hollywood. Known for film-quality haunted houses (often based on horror movies), scare zones and live shows. Universal says it may not be suitable for children under 13. See Universal Halloween Horror Nights.

Knott's Scary Farm (Buena Park, California)

One of the oldest theme-park Halloween events, running since 1973. Known for original mazes, a massive roaming monster cast and a beloved local tradition. Often cited as the event that inspired the theme-park haunt industry.

Busch Gardens' Howl-O-Scream

Busch Gardens parks in Tampa and Williamsburg run Howl-O-Scream nights with haunted houses, scare zones and shows.

Six Flags Fright Fest

Many Six Flags parks host Fright Fest, typically combining family fun by day with mazes and scare zones after dark. Regional parks across the country, from former Cedar Fair parks to independents, run their own Halloween scare nights as well.

How to choose

Ask yourself:

  1. Who's coming? Under 12 → family event. Teens and adults → scare night. Mixed group → a park that does both.
  2. What do they want? Candy and characters, or screaming?
  3. How much do you want to spend? Scare events often require separate tickets and benefit from express passes.
  4. When can you go? Early-season weeknights are cheapest and least crowded.

Tips for both

  • Buy tickets online in advance : popular nights sell out.
  • Arrive early.
  • Bring a bag for treats (family events).
  • Use the park app for wait times.
  • Eat beforehand : food lines grow in the evening.
  • Dress for the weather : California nights get cool; Florida nights stay humid.
  • Read costume rules : allowed at Disney parties, usually not at scare events.

Build a theme-park Halloween trip

Southern California offers Disneyland, Universal Studios Hollywood and Knott's within an hour of each other : see the California Halloween Road Trip. In Florida, combine Orlando's parks with historic St. Augustine. And for a trip built around the scariest attractions, read Planning a Haunted Attraction Road Trip.

Frequently asked questions

Which theme park Halloween event is best for young kids?

Disney's Halloween parties are the most popular family events, but many parks, including SeaWorld, LEGOLAND and regional parks, run daytime Halloween festivals with trick-or-treating, costume parades and gentle shows for young children.

What is the difference between Halloween Horror Nights and Knott's Scary Farm?

Both are after-dark scare events with mazes and scare zones. Halloween Horror Nights at Universal is known for film-quality houses, often based on horror franchises. Knott's Scary Farm in Buena Park, California, has run since 1973 and is known for original mazes, a large roaming monster cast and a strong local tradition.

Are amusement park scare events appropriate for children?

Most after-dark scare events advise that they are not recommended for young children, often citing an age around 13. Some parks run family-friendly daytime Halloween events and switch to scare events at night.

How can I avoid crowds at theme park Halloween events?

Visit on a weeknight early in the season, arrive at opening, buy express or fast-pass upgrades if available, and avoid the final weekend before Halloween, which is usually the busiest.

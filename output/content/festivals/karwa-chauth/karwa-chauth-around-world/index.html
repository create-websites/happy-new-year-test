Our approach to Karwa Chauth, across borders

Karwa Chauth is especially associated with North Indian Hindu family traditions; diaspora and contemporary adaptations differ by region, household, age and individual choice. Related observances such as Teejri, Savitri Brata and Karadaiyan Nombu are discussed as distinct traditions, not renamed Karwa Chauth.

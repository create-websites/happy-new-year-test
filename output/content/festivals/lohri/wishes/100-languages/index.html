Frequently asked questions

What is Lohri and how is it celebrated?

Lohri is a Punjabi harvest festival celebrated on the night before Makar Sankranti, marking the end of winter and the reaping of the rabi crop. Families and communities gather around a bonfire, sing folk songs like Sundar Mundriye, and toss offerings of til, gajak, popcorn, peanuts, and sugarcane into the flames.

How do you say Happy Lohri in different languages?

Happy Lohri is Lohri diyaan lakh lakh vadhaiyaan in Punjabi, Lohri ki hardik shubhkamnaye in Hindi, Shubho Lohri in Bengali, and Lohri Nalvazhthukal in Tamil, alongside translations in Sanskrit, regional Indian languages, and famous world languages.

When is Lohri in 2027?

Lohri falls on Wednesday, January 13, 2027 (some panchangs place the bonfire on January 14). Dates for festivals that follow the moon can differ by a day depending on the calendar and region, so check a local calendar.

How many Indian languages are included for Lohri?

28: all 22 languages on India's Eighth Schedule, plus 6 widely spoken languages that are not on it (Bhojpuri, Rajasthani (Marwari), Tulu, Chhattisgarhi, Magahi, Haryanvi). The remaining 10 cards are world languages.

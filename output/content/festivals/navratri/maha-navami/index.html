About this collection

This rebuilt Maha Navami collection is deliberately text-first. Images are used sparingly, while each language page preserves every supplied greeting and adds four longer wishes for family, friends, colleagues and social sharing.

Some regional-language source files include a native-speaker review note; those notices remain visible on the corresponding pages, and the additional wording is clearly separated from the supplied source wishes.

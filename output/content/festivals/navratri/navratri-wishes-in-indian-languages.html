How do you wish Happy Navratri in Indian languages?

Navratri greetings vary by language, but common themes include the blessings of Maa Durga and her nine forms, peace, prosperity, family well-being, fulfillment of wishes, and the festive joy of Garba and Dandiya. Navratri Wishes and Blessings

These Navratri greetings celebrate devotion to Maa Durga, Ambe, Jagdamba and Bhavani, with wishes for happiness, peace, prosperity, protection and fulfilled aspirations. Several language sections also reflect the festive energy of Garba and Dandiya.

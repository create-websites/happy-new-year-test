About this page

Greetings are shown in each language's own script with a romanised line where helpful. Spelling and wording vary by region, dialect and formality. Cards marked as approximate or built from the language's own words are the least certain, and no page has been reviewed by a speaker of every language. This page was created with AI assistance and published under the byline of Prashant Dhingra.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say Happy Raksha Bandhan in different languages?

Happy Raksha Bandhan is Rakshabandhan ki hardik shubhkamnaye in Hindi, Shubho Rakhi Bandhan in Bengali, Raksha Bandhan Nalvazhthukal in Tamil, Rakshabandhan Shubhakankshalu in Telugu, and Joyeux Raksha Bandhan in French, among many other regional and diaspora variations.

When is Rakhi (Raksha Bandhan) in 2027?

Rakhi (Raksha Bandhan) falls on Tuesday, August 17, 2027. Dates for festivals that follow the moon can differ by a day depending on the calendar and region, so check a local calendar.

How many Indian languages are included for Rakhi (Raksha Bandhan)?

28: all 22 languages on India's Eighth Schedule, plus 6 widely spoken languages that are not on it (Bhojpuri, Rajasthani (Marwari), Tulu, Chhattisgarhi, Magahi, Haryanvi). The remaining 10 cards are world languages.

How do I copy a greeting?

Press the Copy button on a card to copy the greeting in its own script, then paste it into a message or card.

Are the translations reviewed by native speakers?

The greetings are standard or published forms where available, but spelling and register can vary by region and dialect, and the page has not been reviewed by a speaker of every language. Cards marked as approximate are the least certain. If you spot an improvement for your language, please let the author know.

Are there wishes for other festivals?

Yes. The collection covers twelve festivals, from New Year and Valentine's Day to Diwali, Pongal and Christmas, and you can open any of them from the All festival wishes page.

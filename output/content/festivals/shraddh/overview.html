Why Shraddh matters

A ritual language for memory, duty and gratitude

Shraddh gives Hindu families a structured way to remember ancestors through water, food, charity, prayer and family memory. Pitru Paksha creates a dedicated lunar season for that remembrance.

This hub organizes 20 guides into four clusters and deliberately separates traditional belief from fear-based claims, medical advice and scientific overreach.

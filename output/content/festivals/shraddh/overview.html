Why Shraddh matters

A ritual language for memory, duty and gratitude

Shraddh provides Hindu families with a structured means of honoring ancestors through offerings of water, food, charity, prayer, and family reminiscence. Pitru Paksha designates a specific lunar period for this cherished remembrance.

Navigating Awkward Family Dynamics at Thanksgiving

Implement practical hosting boundaries, steer conversations towards neutral topics, and employ de-escalation tactics to minimize political disputes and personal conflicts during Thanksgiving gatherings.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHosting, Prep & Decor

Set expectations before the table gets tense

To prevent family arguments over politics, money, relationships, or parenting, it's best to intervene before Thanksgiving Day. The host can set the tone for the gathering by focusing on reconnecting and kindly requesting that guests steer clear of contentious topics.

It is more palatable to adhere to a boundary that is universal rather than singling out a specific individual's perspective.

Use neutral redirection, not debate tricks

If the conversation gets too intense, steer it towards common experiences like travel, family stories, food, sports, books, children's milestones, or winter plans. If someone tries to bring up politics, politely remind them that the dinner table is not the place for that discussion and suggest they save it for another time.

The aim is not to assert the correctness of any political position, but rather to ensure that the gathering remains respectful.

Give people physical ways to reset

Tasks in the kitchen, taking a brief stroll, playing with kids, or finding a quiet spot can halt a situation from escalating. Keep in mind that alcohol can reduce inhibitions, so make sure there are plenty of non-alcoholic beverages available and avoid using drinking as the main method for handling conflicts.

Know when to stop trying to create harmony

Respectful disagreement is expected, but personal attacks, harassment, or threats are not tolerated. A host has the right to intervene if the conversation becomes unsafe, including ending the discussion, separating guests, or asking someone to leave. Maintaining peace does not mean tolerating abusive behavior.

Frequently asked questions

Should a host ban political conversation?

It varies by the group. Implementing a neutral rule to prevent recurring hostile topics can be justifiable, particularly if it is uniformly enforced.

What is a good phrase for redirecting tension?

Let's not address that during dinner. Instead, let's change the topic and just enjoy each other's company.

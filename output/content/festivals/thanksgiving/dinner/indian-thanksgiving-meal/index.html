Questions

Indian Thanksgiving FAQ

How can Indian families celebrate Thanksgiving with Indian food?

Keep the Thanksgiving structure : centerpiece, starch, sides, condiments and dessert : and fill it with Indian dishes such as tandoori turkey or paneer, biryani, dal, green bean poriyal, naan, cranberry chutney and pumpkin halwa.

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026 : the fourth Thursday of November.

How do I organize an Indian community Thanksgiving potluck?

Assign dishes by category, have the host cover rice, bread, drinks and supplies, label every dish for diet and spice, and plan reheating and leftovers ahead.

Can an Indian Thanksgiving be fully vegetarian?

Yes. Paneer butter masala, malai kofta or a masala-stuffed pumpkin can be the centerpiece, with biryani, dal, vegetables, breads, chutneys and sweets.

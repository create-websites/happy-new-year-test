Upgrading Classic Thanksgiving Sides Without Losing Tradition

Elevate traditional Thanksgiving dishes like stuffing, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, and green bean casserole with modern twists that maintain the holiday spirit.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Keep the memory, change the technique

When it comes to holiday sides, people have high expectations. To give them a modern twist, it's best to keep the traditional flavors intact while incorporating one or two intentional enhancements. Completely changing the dish all at once can make the table seem more like a fancy restaurant menu than a cozy Thanksgiving feast.

Stuffing: build layers of savory flavor

Make sure to toast the bread well to maintain its texture. Cook the onion and celery until they are sweet, then mix in herbs like sage, thyme, or parsley. For a different flavor, think about adding roasted mushrooms, apples, chestnuts, or toasted nuts. Be careful to keep the additional ingredients in proportion so they enhance rather than overpower the bread and herb combination.

Mashed potatoes: texture first

Select starchy potatoes for a fluffy mash, pat them dry post-boiling, and heat butter and milk before combining. Steer clear of over-mixing to prevent a gummy texture. Enhance with browned butter, roasted garlic, or a hint of horseradish for added complexity without straying too far from tradition.

Cranberry sauce and green beans

Cranberry sauce pairs well with tangy and spicy flavors like orange zest, ginger, or a hint of warm spice to enhance the fruit. When making green beans, opt for blanched beans, sautéed mushrooms, and crispy onions instead of canned ingredients to maintain a fresh taste while staying true to the casserole's flavor.

Frequently asked questions

How many side dishes should I make?

Adjust the number of substantial sides, bread, and cranberry sauce for a mixed table based on the number of guests and kitchen capacity. Three to five items is typically sufficient.

Can sides be made ahead?

There are plenty of options for make-ahead dishes, such as cranberry sauce, casseroles, and stuffing components, while mashed potatoes should be reheated with extra care.

Giving Back Locally During Thanksgiving

When supporting food banks, shelters, and delivery programs during Thanksgiving, consider asking organizations for their specific needs and balancing holiday generosity with ongoing assistance throughout the year.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Ask organizations what is actually useful

Before planning a donation drive, be sure to review the specific needs, storage capacities, and volunteer guidelines of food banks and shelters. It may be more cost-effective to donate cash, as organizations can purchase food in bulk at a lower cost than retail shoppers.

Think beyond canned-food drives

Different communities may require various forms of assistance such as hygiene products, transportation aid, meal delivery, winter clothing, gift cards, translation services, or ongoing financial support to be helpful.

Volunteer with humility

Holiday volunteer opportunities fill up fast. If Thanksgiving is already booked, try volunteering before or after, or commit to a regular shift later in the season. Always follow staff guidelines and refrain from taking photos of recipients without their permission.

Make service part of family tradition

Encourage children to assist in organizing pantry-friendly donations, crafting supportive notes for organizations in need, or selecting a shared cause as a family. The aim is to instill a routine of community involvement beyond just one holiday.

Frequently asked questions

Is donating money better than donating food?

What if Thanksgiving volunteer shifts are full?

Consider volunteering on additional days or committing to providing ongoing support. Nonprofits require assistance year-round, not just during holidays.

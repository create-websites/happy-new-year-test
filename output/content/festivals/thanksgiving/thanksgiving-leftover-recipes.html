Thanksgiving Leftovers: Gourmet Transformations

Transform your leftover turkey, stuffing, cranberry sauce, veggies, and pie into soups, sandwiches, casseroles, and other delicious meals, all while ensuring safe storage practices are followed.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Cool and store leftovers first

To kickstart creativity, make sure to store leftovers safely. According to USDA guidelines, it is advised to refrigerate perishable leftovers within two hours of cooking or serving. For quicker cooling, divide large portions into shallow containers.

Thanksgiving leftovers should be consumed within four days if refrigerated, otherwise freeze any leftovers that won't be used within that time frame.

Turkey becomes a new protein

Incorporate sliced turkey into a pressed sandwich paired with cranberry sauce and a bold condiment, mix chopped turkey into enchiladas or pot pie filling, or briefly simmer in a broth-based soup. Remember to portion out what you need to avoid reheating the entire batch multiple times.

Stuffing, potatoes and vegetables

Stuffing can be transformed into crispy patties with an egg on top, a savory waffle, or the foundation of a casserole. Mashed potatoes can be turned into potato cakes or used to thicken soup. Roasted vegetables are great additions to grain bowls, frittatas, and pureed soups.

Desserts can become components

Break up pie into parfaits, transform pumpkin pie filling into a milkshake or yogurt swirl, or freeze for later use. This strategy minimizes waste and turns the day after Thanksgiving into an opportunity for innovative cooking instead of just a repeat of leftovers.

Note: Refrigerate perishable foods immediately; if unsure about storage conditions, throw away instead of trying a sample.

Frequently asked questions

How long are Thanksgiving leftovers safe in the refrigerator?

According to USDA guidelines, Thanksgiving leftovers stored in the refrigerator can be safely consumed within four days if properly chilled and stored.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving leftovers?

Sure. Divide into meal-sized portions, date label, and store in freezer-safe containers for optimal freshness.

Wish 1
Lá an Altaithe sona duit!
Happy Thanksgiving!
Wish 2
Guím Lá an Altaithe te lúcháireach ort.
Wishing you a warm and joyful Thanksgiving.
Wish 3
Go raibh do bhord lán agus do chroí níos lána fós.
May your table be full and your heart even fuller.
Wish 4
Inniu agus i gcónaí, táim buíoch asat.
I am thankful for you today and always.
Wish 5
Lá an Altaithe sona duit féin agus do do theaghlach!
Happy Thanksgiving to you and your family!
Wish 6
Guím lá lán le grá, gáire agus bia blasta ort.
Wishing you a day full of love, laughter and good food.
Wish 7
Go raibh do theach lán le teas agus le buíochas.
May your home be filled with warmth and gratitude.
Wish 8
Go raibh maith agat as gach a dhéanann tú dom.
Thank you for everything you do for me.
Wish 9
Guím beannachtaí gan teorainn ort an Lá an Altaithe seo.
Wishing you blessings beyond measure this Thanksgiving.
Wish 10
Go raibh cúis agat i gcónaí a bheith buíoch.
May you always have reasons to be thankful.
Wish 11
Bain sult as an turcaí, as an bpióg agus as an gcomhluadar!
Enjoy the turkey, the pie and the company!
Wish 12
Guím lá suaimhneach ort leis na daoine a bhfuil grá agat dóibh.
Wishing you a peaceful day with the people you love.
Wish 13
Go dtuga an séasúr buíochais seo áthas duit.
May this season of thanks bring you joy.
Wish 14
Táim buíoch go bhfuil tú i mo shaol.
I am grateful to have you in my life.
Wish 15
Go raibh do bhuaint flúirseach agus do laethanta geal.
May your harvest be rich and your days bright.
Wish 16
Guím sláinte, sonas agus flúirse ort.
Wishing you health, happiness and plenty.
Wish 17
Comhair do bheannachtaí agus bain sult as gach nóiméad.
Count your blessings and enjoy every moment.
Wish 18
Go líona an buíochas do chroí inniu.
May gratitude fill your heart today.
Wish 19
Go raibh maith agat as a bheith i do chara iontach.
Thank you for being a wonderful friend.
Wish 20
Guím Lá an Altaithe compordach ort le do mhuintir agus do chairde.
Wishing you a cozy Thanksgiving with family and friends.
Wish 21
Go raibh do bheannachtaí chomh líonmhar le duilleoga an fhómhair.
May your blessings be as many as the leaves of autumn.
Wish 22
Buíoch as cairde maithe, bia maith agus dea-amanna.
Thankful for good friends, good food and good times.
Wish 23
Guím Lá an Altaithe sona suaimhneach ar mo chomhghleacaithe.
Wishing my colleagues a happy and restful Thanksgiving.
Wish 24
Go raibh do phláta lán agus do bhuairt gann.
May your plate be full and your worries few.
Wish 25
Gabhaimis buíochas as gach a bhfuil againn.
Let's give thanks for all we have.
Wish 26
Seolaim barróg the chugat an Lá an Altaithe seo.
Sending you warm hugs this Thanksgiving.
Wish 27
Gach dea-ghuí do Lá an Altaithe sona!
Best wishes for a happy Thanksgiving!

About this page

Greetings are shown in each language's own script with a romanised line where helpful. Spelling and wording vary by region, dialect and formality. Cards marked as approximate or built from the language's own words are the least certain, and no page has been reviewed by a speaker of every language. This page was created with AI assistance and published under the byline of Happy New Year 2027.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say Happy Thanksgiving in different languages?

Happy Thanksgiving translates as Joyeux Thanksgiving in French, Feliz Dia de Accion de Gracias in Spanish, Gan'en jie kuaile in Mandarin Chinese, and Frohes Erntedankfest in German, among dozens of other regional variations built around the universal idea of giving thanks.

Is Thanksgiving celebrated outside the United States and Canada?

The specific American and Canadian holiday of Thanksgiving is not widely celebrated elsewhere, but nearly every culture has its own harvest festival or day of gratitude, from Germany's Erntedankfest to Japan's Kinro Kansha no Hi, which is why gratitude-themed greetings exist in so many languages.

When is Thanksgiving in 2027?

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 25, 2027 (United States).

How do I copy a greeting?

Press the Copy button on a card to copy the greeting in its own script, then paste it into a message or card.

Are the translations reviewed by native speakers?

The greetings are standard or published forms where available, but spelling and register can vary by region and dialect, and the page has not been reviewed by a speaker of every language. Cards marked as approximate are the least certain. If you spot an improvement for your language, please let the author know.

About this page

Greetings are shown in each language's own script with a romanised line where helpful. Spelling and wording vary by region, dialect and formality. Cards marked as approximate or built from the language's own words are the least certain, and no page has been reviewed by a speaker of every language. Frequently asked questions

How do you say Happy Valentine's Day in different languages?

Happy Valentine's Day translates differently across languages, Joyeuse Saint-Valentin in French, Feliz Dia de San Valentin in Spanish, Barentainu dee omedetou in Japanese, and Sevgililer Gunun Kutlu Olsun in Turkish, among dozens of regional variations collected here.

Do all countries celebrate Valentine's Day as a romantic holiday?

Not exactly. While most countries treat February 14 as a day for romantic partners, Estonia and Finland both call it Friend's Day, sobrapaev and ystavanpaiva respectively, celebrating friendship and platonic bonds rather than romance alone.

When is Valentine's Day in 2027?

Valentine's Day falls on Sunday, February 14, 2027.

How do I copy a greeting?

Press the Copy button on a card to copy the greeting in its own script, then paste it into a message or card.

Are the translations reviewed by native speakers?

The greetings are standard or published forms where available, but spelling and register can vary by region and dialect, and the page has not been reviewed by a speaker of every language. Cards marked as approximate are the least certain. If you spot an improvement for your language, please let the author know.

Yes. The collection covers twelve festivals, from New Year and Valentine's Day to Diwali, Pongal and Christmas, and you can open any of them from the All festival wishes page.

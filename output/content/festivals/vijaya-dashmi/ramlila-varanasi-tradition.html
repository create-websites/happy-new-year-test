Ramlila of Varanasi: Open-Air Theatre and Living Tradition

Understand the open-air Ramlila traditions associated with Varanasi, their UNESCO-recognized heritage context, and why audiences move through the story as a community.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

Ramlila is not simply a stage show. In long-running traditions, entire neighborhoods and public spaces can become part of the narrative landscape.

The Ramayana performed in public space

Ramlila dramatizes the Ramayana over multiple evenings, often culminating around Dussehra. In the Varanasi region, famous open-air traditions use streets, grounds and dispersed locations rather than a single conventional theatre.

The audience may physically move with the action, turning spectatorship into pilgrimage-like participation.

UNESCO-recognized heritage context

The editorial brief points to UNESCO-recognized Ramlila traditions. That recognition highlights Ramlila as a living performance tradition carried through oral recitation, acting, music, costume, community organization and inherited practice.

Its cultural value lies in transmission from generation to generation, not only in the final performance.

Why place matters

When a scene unfolds in an actual public landscape, geography becomes part of storytelling. One area may stand in for Ayodhya, another for a forest or battlefield, and movement between them gives the narrative physical scale.

This is one reason long-form Ramlila can feel immersive even without modern theatrical technology.

Watching respectfully

Visitors should expect devotional seriousness alongside performance. Avoid blocking processions, follow local photography norms and remember that many participants experience the event as worship as well as theatre.

The best way to understand it is to watch more than the climactic scenes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Is Ramlila only performed on Dussehra day?

No. Many traditions unfold across several days or weeks and culminate around Dussehra.

Why is the Varanasi tradition distinctive?

Open-air, multi-location performance makes the city landscape part of the narrative.

Is Ramlila theatre or worship?

It can function as both, depending on the community and participants.

A tradition that travels

One New Year, Many Ways to Celebrate

The countdown is familiar, but the customs are wonderfully different. In Japan, many people mark the season with temple visits and joya no kane, the symbolic ringing of temple bells. In Spain, eating twelve grapes at midnight is a much-loved good-luck tradition. New York is known for its Times Square ball drop, while Australia welcomes the new year with dramatic harbour celebrations.

The Human Impulse for a New Beginning

The New Year’s turn is a universal event crossing cultures, lands, and eras. It symbolizes humanity's shared desire to organize time, let go of the past, and envision a better future. This journey delves into its origins, from ancient farming cycles to today’s global yet unique festivities. Discover the traditions, meanings, and enduring hope for renewal that unite us all.

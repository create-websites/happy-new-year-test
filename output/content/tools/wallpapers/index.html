How to set your wallpaper

iPhone

Save the image to Photos. Open Settings, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper and choose Photos. Pick the image, adjust it and tap Add.

Android

Open the image in Google Photos or your gallery app, tap the three-dot menu, choose Use as, then Wallpaper. Menu names vary by phone maker.

Windows

Right-click the downloaded file and choose Set as desktop background, or go to Settings, Personalization, Background.

Mac

Open System Settings, click Wallpaper, then Add Photo and pick the file. You can also right-click it in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture.

Questions

Are these New Year wallpapers free?

Yes. Download as many as you like for your own phone, tablet or computer.

Which size should I pick?

Choose This screen to match the device you are using right now. Otherwise pick your phone's screen size, a tablet, or a desktop resolution such as 4K.

Will the lock-screen clock cover the design?

Phone designs keep the main artwork below the clock area. Turn on Lock screen preview to check before you download.

Can I add my name?

Yes. Type a name or a word for the year in Your text and it is drawn into the wallpaper.

Why is every wallpaper a little different when I shuffle?

The artwork is drawn fresh in your browser. Shuffle layout rearranges the fireworks and confetti; the colours and design stay the same.

What file format do I get?

A PNG at the exact resolution you chose, so it stays sharp on high-resolution screens.

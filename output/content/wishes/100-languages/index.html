About this page

Greetings are shown in each language's own script with a romanised line where helpful. Spelling and wording vary by region, dialect and formality. Cards marked as approximate or built from the language's own words are the least certain, and no page has been reviewed by a speaker of every language. This page was created with AI assistance and published under the byline of Prashant Dhingra.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say Happy New Year in different languages?

It changes with language and script: Bonne Année in French, ¡Feliz Año Nuevo! in Spanish, 新年快乐 (Xīnnián kuàilè) in Mandarin, 明けましておめでとうございます in Japanese and नया साल मुबारक हो in Hindi. This page lists 100 greetings, grouped by region, and each one can be copied with one click.

Which place celebrates New Year first?

The Line Islands of Kiribati, at UTC+14, see the new year first. Samoa, Tonga and Tokelau at UTC+13 follow, then New Zealand in summer time and Fiji, and midnight then travels west through Asia, Africa and Europe to the Americas.

Is the order on this page exact?

No. Regions run roughly in the order midnight reaches them, but several regions overlap in time zone, so the order is approximate. The UTC range shown with each region is a guide only.

Do all of these languages celebrate on January 1?

Not all. Chinese, Vietnamese, Tibetan, Korean and Mongolian speakers also mark a lunar new year, Persian speakers celebrate Nowruz in spring, and the Hebrew greeting here is for the civil new year on January 1. The 2027 Festival Countdown shows when Chinese New Year and Seollal fall.

Why are some greetings marked as written or short forms?

Tongan is usually written rather than spoken, and published Fijian and Chichewa phrase lists give particular forms. The cards for those languages say so.

How do I copy a greeting?

Press the Copy button on a card to copy the greeting in its own script, then paste it into a message or card.

Are the translations reviewed by native speakers?

The greetings are standard published forms, but spelling and register can vary by region and dialect, and the page has not been reviewed by a speaker of every language. If you spot an improvement for your language, please let the author know.

Where can I find holidays and festivals for 2027?

The 2027 World Holidays page lets you pick a region, country or month, and the 2027 Festival Countdown shows live timers by region.

When is New Year in 2027?

New Year falls on Friday, January 1, 2027.

Are there wishes for other festivals?

Yes. The collection covers twelve festivals, from New Year and Valentine's Day to Diwali, Pongal and Christmas, and you can open any of them from the All festival wishes page.
